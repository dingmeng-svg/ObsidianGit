My most treasured darling, though restless waters flow between the shores where dawn first touches my thoughts and the distant harbor where your gentle spirit dwells, my heart navigates these currents like an ancient mariner following stars that never fade.

From this realm where glass towers pierce the morning mist, where innovation blooms like wildflowers in concrete gardens, I send my devotion across the singing strait to where you reside among hills crowned with temple bells and streets that whisper stories of old dynasties. Like Wordsworth's solitary reaper whose song echoes across Highland valleys, my love reverberates through the chambers of memory, transforming ordinary moments into golden verses.

You are my compass rose in this vast ocean of longing, more luminous than the constellation that guides ships through midnight waters, more precious than pearls gathered from the deepest currents. Though geography may sketch its lines upon our earthly maps, know that in the boundless territory of the heart, distance dissolves like morning mist, and you remain forever present in the sanctuary of my devotion.

Until fate weaves our separate threads into one tapestry, I remain, with affection as constant as the eternal dialogue between moon and tide, your devoted soul from across the ever-singing waters.

## 附注

华兹华斯在《抒情歌谣集》序言中阐述了浪漫主义诗歌的核心理念：“强烈情感的自然流露”需要通过深思熟虑的意象选择来实现。英国浪漫主义诗人善于运用暗喻手法，“将抽象的爱转化为具体的事物”，同时保持其象征意义的开放性和多重性。
