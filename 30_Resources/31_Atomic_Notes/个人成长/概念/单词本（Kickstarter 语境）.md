## 例句

**hype**
> As a creator, you can set up a pre-launch page to share essential information and start building hype for your campaign.

**curating**
> We recommend curating your pre-launch content to generate excitement instead of simply copying your campaign page.

**monetization**
> Relying only on ad revenue and platform-specific monetization leaves creators wanting more sustainable ways for financial growth.

**bootstrapping**
> Turning bold ideas into reality takes capital — a challenge that often forces creators to deal with bootstrapping, loans, or equity financing.

**dilute**
> These paths can dilute ownership and impose rigid structures that may hold back the creative process.

**exemplify**
> Chris's campaign exemplifies the power of Kickstarter as a proven platform for creators.
