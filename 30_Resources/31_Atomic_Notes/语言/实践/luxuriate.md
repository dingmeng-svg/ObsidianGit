## 词典查证
**音标**（Cambridge Dictionary）
英 /lʌɡˈʒʊə.ri.eɪt/ 美 /lʌɡˈʒʊr.i.eɪt/

**核心定义**（Collins Dictionary）
1. **尽情享受**
   To enjoy something very much
2. **沉溺于**
   To take pleasure in a comfortable situation

**典型例句**：
- She luxuriated in the warm bath.
- He luxuriated in his newfound freedom.
