## 词典查证
**音标**（Cambridge Dictionary）
- buzz: /bʌz/ UK, /bʌz/ US
- paparazzi: /ˌpæp.əˈræt.si/ UK, /ˌpɑː.pəˈrɑː.tsi/ US
- bee: /biː/ UK, /biː/ US

**核心定义**（Collins Dictionary）
- **buzz** v. If something buzzes or buzzes somewhere, it makes a long, continuous sound like a bee. 发出嗡嗡声
- **paparazzi** n. Paparazzi are photographers who follow famous people around and take photographs of them, especially when they are doing something they would rather keep private. 狗仔队
- **bee** n. A bee is an insect with wings that makes honey. 蜜蜂

**搭配结构**：a + buzzing + paparazzi + of + bees（名词性比喻短语）
- 结构模式：a + adj. + collective-noun + of + plural-noun（群像比喻）
- 修辞手法：隐喻（metaphor）— 将蜜蜂群比作狗仔队，强调其围聚、喧嚣、无孔不入的特征

**近义词网络**（Thesaurus）
- swarm of bees / cloud of bees / hive of bees（同义群像）
- a flock of / a herd of / a pack of（其他群像结构）
- buzzing → humming / droning / whirring（声音近义）
- paparazzi → press corps / media scrum / reporters（近义群体）

**典型搭配**
- a buzzing paparazzi of bees（原句搭配）
- a buzzing swarm of insects / a buzzing cloud of flies（类推搭配）
- a paparazzi of photographers / a paparazzi of fans（类推搭配）

**原文补充**（阅读笔记）
Peterson 用此比喻描述蜜蜂群围聚的景象，将蜜蜂的嗡嗡声和密集围攻与狗仔队的特征叠加，形成生动的视觉+听觉双重意象。
