## 词典查证
**音标**（Cambridge Dictionary）
英式 /ˈklæm.ər/；美式 /ˈklæm.ər/

**核心定义**（Collins Dictionary）
1. **N-UNCOUNT** Clamour is the loud and often continuous noise made by a large group of people.
   *There was a clamour of voices in the hall.*
2. **N-UNCOUNT** A clamour is a demand or protest made by a lot of people.
   *There was a clamour for his resignation.*
3. **VERB** If people clamour for something, they make a loud and angry demand for it.
   *The crowd clamoured for the performer to appear.*

**原文补充**（阅读笔记）

**音标**：/ˈklæmər/

**释义**：大声或愤怒地持续要求；大量人群提出的诉求或抱怨

**典型用法**：clamour for sth; clamour to do sth

**语义关系**：
- **近义链**：clamour（愤怒持续要求）→ outcry（强烈抗议）→ uproar（激烈骚动）→ din（持续嘈杂声）
- **强度链**：uproar（最激烈骚动）> clamour（持续愤怒要求）> outcry（强烈抗议）> fuss（小规模抱怨）
- **反义链**：clamour ↔ silence, quiet, calm, stillness
- **对比对象**：[[clamour]]（强调"持续、愤怒、大量人群"）vs [[outcry]]（单次强烈抗议）vs [[fuss]]（小规模、情绪化抱怨）
