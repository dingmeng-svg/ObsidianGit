## 词典查证
**音标**（Cambridge Dictionary）
英 /spiːk jɔː maɪnd/  美 /spiːk jʊr maɪnd/

**核心定义**（Collins Dictionary）
1. **直言不讳**
   To express one's opinions openly
2. **坦率表达**
   To say what one really thinks
3. **说出想法**
   To speak honestly without holding back

**典型例句**：
- Don't hesitate to speak your mind.
- She always speaks her mind.
- It's important to speak your mind in meetings.
