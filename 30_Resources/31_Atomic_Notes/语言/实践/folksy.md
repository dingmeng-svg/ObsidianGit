## 词典查证
**音标**（Cambridge Dictionary）
英 /ˈfəʊk.si/  美 /ˈfoʊk.si/

**核心定义**（Collins Dictionary）
1. **质朴的，朴实亲切的**
   If you describe someone as folksy, you mean that they are friendly and informal in manner. (COBUILD)
2. **民间风格的，乡土的**
   Folksy things are characteristic of traditional rural culture, especially in the US.
3. **adj.** simple, warm, and unpretentious in a way that suggests traditional rural values.

**典型例句**：
- He has a folksy speaking style that puts people at ease.
- The candidate adopted a folksy approach to connect with rural voters.
- The restaurant had a folksy charm with its wooden decor and checkered tablecloths.
- Her folksy wisdom resonated with the small-town audience.

## 正文
folksy 是《12 Rules for Life》阅读过程中遇到的词汇。

**核心含义**：质朴的，朴实亲切的；民间风格的

**文化语境**：folksy 在美国文化中带有"乡土/朴实/亲切"的正面含义，常用于形容政治家刻意展现的"接地气"风格，或形容温暖、不矫饰的人际风格。

## 强对话链接
**语义场**：[[个性特征]]

**近义词网络**（Thesaurus）

- **[[folksy]]** → down-to-earth, unpretentious, homey
- **[[down-to-earth]]** → folksy, practical, realistic
- **[[unpretentious]]** → folksy, modest, simple

**语义关系**：
- folksy vs down-to-earth：folksy 带风格化意味（可暗示刻意），down-to-earth 更真诚
- folksy vs unpretentious：folksy 强调"乡土亲切感"，unpretentious 强调"不矫饰"
- folksy vs homey：folksy 偏人际风格，homey 偏环境氛围（家的感觉）
- folksy ↔ folk：folk=民间/民众（名词/形容词），folksy=民间风格的（只作形容词，含褒贬双重性）
