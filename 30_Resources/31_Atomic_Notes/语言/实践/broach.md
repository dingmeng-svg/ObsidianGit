## 词典查证
**音标**（Cambridge Dictionary）
英式 /brəʊtʃ/；美式 /broʊtʃ/

**核心定义**（Collins Dictionary）
1. **VERB** When you broach a subject, especially a sensitive one, you mention it in order to start a discussion.
   *I broached the subject of his drinking with him.*
2. **VERB** [archaic] To broach a cask or bottle means to open it, especially to drink from it.
   *They broached another barrel of ale.*
