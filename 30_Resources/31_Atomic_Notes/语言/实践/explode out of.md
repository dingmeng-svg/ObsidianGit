## 词典查证
**音标**（Cambridge Dictionary）
explode /ɪkˈspləʊd/ (UK) /ɪkˈsploʊd/ (US)

**核心定义**（Collins Dictionary）
1. **explode** v. to burst or cause to burst with a loud noise; to increase suddenly and rapidly. (COBUILD)
2. **explode out of** (phrasal collocation): to burst forcefully out of something; to emerge or break out with sudden violent energy.

**典型例句**：
- The crowd exploded out of the stadium after the final whistle.
- Anger exploded out of him before he could stop it.
- The chick explodes out of its shell after hours of struggling.

## 正文
explode out of 是《12 Rules for Life》阅读过程中遇到的短语/搭配。

**核心含义**：从…中爆发出来
**典型用法**：explode out of + 名词 — 强调突然、猛烈地从某处冲出/爆发，常用于物理动作或情感宣泄，比 burst out of 更具爆炸性力度

## 强对话链接
**近义词网络**（Thesaurus）

- **[[explode out of]]** → burst out of, erupt from, break out of, blast out of
- **[[burst out of]]** → explode out of, rush out of, storm out of
- **[[erupt from]]** → explode out of, gush from, spew from
- **[[break out of]]** → escape from, force one's way out of, smash out of

**语义关系**：
- explode out of vs burst out of：explode 更猛烈/更具破坏性，burst 更通用
- explode out of vs break out of：explode 强调爆发力，break 强调突破限制
- explode out of vs erupt from：erupt 偏自然/火山意象，explode 偏人为/机械意象

**典型搭配**：
- explode out of the starting blocks（从起跑线猛冲）
- explode out of the room（从房间冲出）
- explode out of its shell（从壳中爆出 — 生命意象）
- anger exploded out of him（怒火从他体内爆发）
