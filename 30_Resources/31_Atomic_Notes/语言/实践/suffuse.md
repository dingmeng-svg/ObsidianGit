## 词典查证
**音标**（Cambridge Dictionary）
英 /səˈfjuːz/  美 /səˈfjuːz/

**核心定义**（Collins Dictionary）
1. **弥漫于**
   To spread through or over something completely
2. **充满于**
   To fill something with a particular quality or emotion

**典型例句**：
- The room was suffused with warm light.
- Her voice was suffused with emotion.
