## 词典查证
**音标**（Cambridge Dictionary）
英 /priːn/  美 /priːn/

**核心定义**（Collins Dictionary）
1. **整理羽毛**
   To clean and arrange feathers with the beak
2. **打扮**
   To spend time making oneself look attractive
3. **自我欣赏**
   To show satisfaction with oneself

**典型例句**：
- The bird preened its feathers.
- She preened herself before the mirror.
- He preened with satisfaction.
