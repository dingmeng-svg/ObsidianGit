**音标**（Cambridge Dictionary）

**核心定义**（Collins Dictionary）
1. **陷入遗忘**
   To be completely forgotten
2. **湮没无闻**
   To fade from memory or awareness
3. **消失**
   To pass into obscurity

**典型例句**：
- The old traditions sank into oblivion.
- His name sank into oblivion.
- Let the past sink into oblivion.
