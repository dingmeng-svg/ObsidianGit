## 词典查证
**音标**（Cambridge Dictionary）
英式 /dɪˈskləʊ.ʒər/；美式 /dɪˈskləʊ.ʒər/

**核心定义**（Collins Dictionary）
1. **N-UNCOUNT** Disclosure is the act of giving people new information.
   *Full disclosure is required for legal compliance.*
2. **N-COUNT** A disclosure is a fact that is made known to people.
   *The disclosure of his financial records caused controversy.*
3. **N-UNCOUNT** [legal] Disclosure is the process of revealing evidence or information.
   *The court ordered full disclosure of all documents.*
