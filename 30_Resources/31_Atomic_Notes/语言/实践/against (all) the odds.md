## 词典查证
**音标**（Cambridge Dictionary）
- against: /əˈɡenst/ UK, /əˈɡenst/ US
- odds: /ɒdz/ UK, /ɑːdz/ US

**核心定义**（Collins Dictionary）
- **against all the odds / against the odds** phrase. If you do something against all the odds, you do it although there were many difficulties and it seemed impossible to do. 尽管困难重重；在极不利的情况下

**近义词网络**（Thesaurus）
- against all the odds → in spite of everything / despite all difficulties / come what may / against the grain（近义短语）
- against the odds → defying expectations / beating the odds / in the face of adversity（近义表达）
- odds → probabilities / chances / likelihood（近义名词）

**典型搭配**
- succeed against all the odds（在困难重重中成功）
- survive against the odds（在逆境中幸存）
- win against all the odds（在极不利情况下获胜）
- against overwhelming odds（面对压倒性的困难）
- the odds are against someone（形势对某人不利）
- beat the odds（战胜困难）

**原文补充**（阅读笔记）
Peterson 用此短语描述人在极端不利条件下仍坚持行动的精神，与书中"在混乱中寻找秩序"的核心主题呼应。
