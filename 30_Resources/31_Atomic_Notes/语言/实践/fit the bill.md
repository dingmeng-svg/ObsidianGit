## 词典查证
**音标**（Cambridge Dictionary）
fit /fɪt/；the /ðə/；bill /bɪl/

**核心定义**（Collins Dictionary）
1. **fit the bill** (idiom): to be exactly what is needed or suitable for a particular purpose. (COBUILD)
2. If someone or something fits the bill, they are exactly what is required or wanted.

**典型例句**：
- This new software fits the bill perfectly for our needs.
- We need someone with leadership experience — she fits the bill.
- None of the candidates really fit the bill.

## 类型
短语/搭配

## 正文
fit the bill 是《12 Rules for Life》阅读过程中遇到的短语/搭配。

**核心含义**：符合要求，适合需要
**典型用法**：fit the bill — 习语，表示"恰好满足需要/完全符合要求"，常用于筛选/选择场景，偏非正式口语

## 强对话链接
**近义词网络**（Thesaurus）

- **[[fit the bill]]** → fill the bill, serve the purpose, do the trick, hit the spot
- **[[serve the purpose]]** → fit the bill, answer the purpose, meet the need
- **[[do the trick]]** → fit the bill, work, do the job, solve the problem
- **[[fill the bill]]** → fit the bill（变体，同义）

**语义关系**：
- fit the bill vs serve the purpose：fit the bill 更口语/非正式，serve the purpose 更正式
- fit the bill vs do the trick：do the trick 偏"解决问题"，fit the bill 偏"符合要求"
- fit the bill vs hit the spot：hit the spot 偏感官满足（食物/舒适），fit the bill 偏功能匹配
- fit the bill = fill the bill：同义变体，fit 更常用

**典型搭配**：
- fit the bill perfectly（完全符合要求）
- fit the bill nicely（很适合）
- doesn't quite fit the bill（不太符合要求）
- exactly fits the bill（恰好符合）
