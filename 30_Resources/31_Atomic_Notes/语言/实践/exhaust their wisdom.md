## 词典查证
**音标**（Cambridge Dictionary）
exhaust /ɪɡˈzɔːst/ (UK) /ɪɡˈzɑːst/ (US)；wisdom /ˈwɪz.dəm/

**核心定义**（Collins Dictionary）
1. **exhaust** v. to use something up completely; to drain all the resources of something. (COBUILD)
2. **wisdom** n. the quality of having experience, knowledge, and good judgement.
3. **exhaust their wisdom** (collocation): to use up or deplete all of someone's knowledge/insight; to have drawn every possible lesson from their experience.

**典型例句**：
- We've exhausted their wisdom on this matter and must seek new counsel.
- The elders' wisdom was exhausted after decades of guiding the community.
- Don't exhaust your wisdom on those who won't listen.

## 正文
exhaust their wisdom 是《12 Rules for Life》阅读过程中遇到的短语/搭配。

**核心含义**：耗尽他们的智慧
**典型用法**：exhaust + one's + wisdom/patience/resources — 表示"用尽某人的某项资源"，常用于正式/学术语境，暗示从某处汲取了所有可用知识后需另寻出路

## 强对话链接
**近义词网络**（Thesaurus）

- **[[exhaust their wisdom]]** → drain their knowledge, deplete their insight, use up their expertise
- **[[exhaust]]** → drain, deplete, consume, use up, expend, sap
- **[[wisdom]]** → insight, sagacity, discernment, prudence, knowledge

**语义关系**：
- exhaust their wisdom vs tap their wisdom：exhaust=用尽（终点），tap=汲取（过程）
- exhaust their wisdom vs run out of wisdom：exhaust 更主动/及物，run out 更被动/不及物
- exhaust + 抽象名词：exhaust one's patience/resources/options/possibilities

**典型搭配**：
- exhaust one's wisdom（耗尽智慧）
- exhaust one's patience（耗尽耐心）
- exhaust all possibilities（穷尽所有可能）
- exhaust the available resources（用尽可用资源）
