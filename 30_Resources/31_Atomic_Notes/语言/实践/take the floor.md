## 词典查证
**音标**（Cambridge Dictionary）
—

**核心定义**（Collins Dictionary）
1. **开始发言**
   To begin speaking in a formal setting
2. **起立发言**
   To stand up to speak

**典型例句**：
- The senator took the floor.
- I'd like to take the floor now.
