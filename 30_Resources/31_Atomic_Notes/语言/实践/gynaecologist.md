**核心定义**（Collins Dictionary）
1. **妇科医生**
   A gynaecologist is a doctor who specializes in treating diseases of the female reproductive system. (COBUILD)
2. A physician who specializes in the medical care of women, particularly their reproductive systems.

**典型例句**：

## 强对话链接
**语义场**：[[医疗职业]]

**近义词网络**（Thesaurus）

- **[[gynaecologist]]** → obstetrician, OB-GYN, women's health specialist
- **[[obstetrician]]** → gynaecologist, midwife, delivery doctor
- **[[OB-GYN]]** → gynaecologist, obstetrician（合称，常见用法）

**语义关系**：
- gynaecologist vs obstetrician：gynaecologist 专妇科疾病，obstetrician 专产科/分娩；OB-GYN = 二者兼修
- gynaecologist vs midwife：gynaecologist 是医生（MD），midwife 是助产士（护士层级）
- gynaecology（妇科）vs obstetrics（产科）：妇科非孕产，产科专注孕产

**典型搭配**：
- see a gynaecologist（看妇科医生）
- visit a gynaecologist（就诊妇科）
- gynaecologist appointment（妇科预约）
- consultant gynaecologist（妇科顾问医生 — 英式）
