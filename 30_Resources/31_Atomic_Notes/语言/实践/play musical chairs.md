**核心定义**（Collins Dictionary）
1. **抢椅子游戏**
   A game where players compete for limited seats
2. **比喻争抢**
   Metaphor for competing for limited resources or positions

**典型例句**：
- Corporate musical chairs with executives.
- Political musical chairs in cabinet reshuffles.
- The job market is like musical chairs.
