## 词典查证
**音标**（Cambridge Dictionary）
英 /pɪk ʌp ɒn/  美 /pɪk ʌp ɑːn/

**核心定义**（Collins Dictionary）
1. **注意到**
   To notice or become aware of something
2. **领会**
   To understand something not directly expressed
3. **察觉**
   To detect subtle signals

**典型例句**：
- She picked up on his hesitation.
- I picked up on the hint.
- He didn't pick up on the joke.
