## 词典查证
**音标**（Cambridge Dictionary）
—（短语搭配，不标音标）

**核心定义**（Collins Dictionary）
1. **PHRASE** To bring something to bear means to use it effectively to achieve something.
   *He brought all his experience to bear on the problem.*
2. **PHRASE** To bring pressure to bear on someone means to use your influence to force them to do something.
   *They brought pressure to bear on the government to change the law.*

## 正文
bring to bear 是《12 Rules for Life》阅读过程中遇到的短语/搭配。

**核心含义**：有效地运用，施加（影响、压力等）
**典型用法**：
- bring pressure to bear (on) — 施加压力
- bring influence to bear — 运用影响力
- bring something to bear (on/upon) — 将某物应用于
