## 词典查证
**音标**（Cambridge Dictionary）
英 /kənˈdjuː.sɪv/  美 /kənˈduː.sɪv/

**核心定义**（Collins Dictionary）
1. **有助于的**
   Making it easy for something to happen
2. **有利的**
   Tending to bring about

**典型例句**：
- A quiet environment is conducive to studying.
- The atmosphere was not conducive.
