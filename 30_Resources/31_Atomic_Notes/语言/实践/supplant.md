## 词典查证
**音标**（Cambridge Dictionary）
英 /səˈplɑːnt/  美 /səˈplænt/

**核心定义**（Collins Dictionary）
1. **取代**
   To replace something or someone
2. **替代**
   To take the place of a person or thing that was in a position before

**典型例句**：
- Television has supplanted radio as our main source of entertainment.
- She was supplanted by a younger colleague.
