## 词典查证
**音标**（Cambridge Dictionary）
英 /ˌʌn.ɪnˈhɪb.ɪ.tɪd/  美 /ˌʌn.ɪnˈhɪb.ɪ.tɪd/

**核心定义**（Collins Dictionary）
1. **不拘束的**
   Free and natural, not feeling or showing inhibition
2. **自由的**
   Not held back by social conventions

**典型例句**：
- She gave an uninhibited performance.
- His uninhibited laughter filled the room.
