## 提纯

## 词典查证
**音标**（Cambridge Dictionary）
英式 /dʌl/；美式 /dʌl/

**核心定义**（Collins Dictionary）
1. **ADJ** If something is dull, it is not interesting or exciting.
   *The lecture was dull and boring.*
2. **ADJ** If something is dull, it is not very bright or shiny.
   *The dull grey sky made him feel depressed.*
3. **ADJ** If a knife or blade is dull, it is not sharp.
   *Use a sharp knife - this one is too dull.*
4. **ADJ** If someone is dull, they are not very intelligent or lively.
   *He was a dull, unimaginative man.*
5. **ADJ** A dull pain is not very sharp, but it continues for a long time.
   *She felt a dull ache in her lower back.*

**原文补充**（阅读笔记）

**音标**：/dʌl/

**释义**：乏味的，无趣的

**典型用法**：dull conversation; dull knife; dull pain

## 正文
dull 是《12 Rules for Life》阅读过程中遇到的词汇。

**核心含义**：乏味的，无趣的；但 dull 有多义性：乏味/迟钝/暗淡/不锋利/钝痛
