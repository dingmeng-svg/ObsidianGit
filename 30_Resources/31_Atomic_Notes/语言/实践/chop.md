## 词典查证
**音标**（Cambridge Dictionary）
英式 /tʃɒp/；美式 /tʃɑːp/

**核心定义**（Collins Dictionary）
1. **VERB** If you chop something, you cut it into pieces with strong, downward movements of a knife or an axe.
   *He was chopping wood for the fire.*
2. **VERB** If you chop something such as vegetables or meat, you cut them into small pieces.
   *Chop the onions finely.*
3. **N-COUNT** A chop is a small piece of meat cut from the rib of an animal.
   *We had lamb chops for dinner.*
4. **N-SING** [informal] A chop is a sharp downward blow or stroke.
   *He gave the log a single chop and it split in two.*
