## 词典查证
**音标**（Cambridge Dictionary）
英 /ɪnˈskraɪb/ 美 /ɪnˈskraɪb/

**核心定义**（Collins Dictionary）
1. **刻写**
   To write or carve words or symbols on a surface
2. **题赠**
   To write a dedication in a book

**典型例句**：
- He inscribed his name in the book.
- The monument was inscribed with the names of the fallen.
