**核心定义**（Collins Dictionary）
1. **摇摆**
   To move something from side to side (especially a tail)
2. **逃学**
   To stay away from school without permission (British slang)

**典型例句**：
- The dog wagged its tail happily.
- He was caught wagging it again.
