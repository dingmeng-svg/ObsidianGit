## 词典查证
**音标**（Cambridge Dictionary）
英 /ˈped.ənt/  美 /ˈped.ənt/

**核心定义**（Collins Dictionary）
1. **学究**
   A person who pays excessive attention to rules and details
2. **迂腐之人**
   Someone overly concerned with formal rules

**典型例句**：
- He's such a pedant about grammar.
- Don't be a pedant about minor details.
