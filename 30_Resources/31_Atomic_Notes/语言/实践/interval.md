## 词典查证
**音标**（Cambridge Dictionary）
英 /ˈɪn.tə.vəl/ 美 /ˈɪn.t̬ɚ.vəl/

**核心定义**（Collins Dictionary）
1. **间隔**
   A period of time between events
2. **中场休息**
   A short break between parts of a performance

**典型例句**：
- The intervals between the notes were perfectly timed.
- Trains depart at regular intervals.

**原文补充**（阅读笔记）
