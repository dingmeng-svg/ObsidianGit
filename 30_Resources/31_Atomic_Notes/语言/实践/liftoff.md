## 词典查证
**音标**（Cambridge Dictionary）
英 /ˈlɪft.ɒf/ 美 /ˈlɪft.ɑːf/

**核心定义**（Collins Dictionary）
1. **升空**
   The moment when a spacecraft leaves the ground
2. **起飞**
   The action of rising into the air

**典型例句**：
- The rocket's liftoff was successful.
- We have liftoff in T-minus 10 seconds.
