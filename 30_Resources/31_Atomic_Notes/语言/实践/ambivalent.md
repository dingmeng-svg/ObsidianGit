## 词典查证
**音标**（Cambridge Dictionary）
英 /æmˈbɪv.ə.lənt/  美 /æmˈbɪv.ə.lənt/

**核心定义**（Collins Dictionary）
1. **矛盾的，模棱两可的**
   If you are ambivalent about something, you feel two different ways about it.
2. **（情感）矛盾的**
   Having mixed feelings or contradictory ideas about something or someone.

**典型例句**：
- She remains ambivalent about marriage.
- He has an ambivalent attitude towards technology.
- Many people feel ambivalent about the benefits of genetic engineering.
