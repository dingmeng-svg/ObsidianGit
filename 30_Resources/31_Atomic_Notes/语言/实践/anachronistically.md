**音标**（Cambridge Dictionary）

**核心定义**（Collins Dictionary）
1. **时代错误地**
   In a way that involves something being in the wrong historical period.
2. **不合时宜地**
   In a manner that seems old-fashioned or out of place in modern times.

**典型例句**：
- The ideas were anachronistically applied to modern problems.
- He dressed anachronistically in Victorian clothing.
- The film feels anachronistically slow by contemporary standards.
