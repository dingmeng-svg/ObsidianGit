## 词典查证
**音标**（Cambridge Dictionary）
英 /ˈæɡ.ə.naɪz/  美 /ˈæɡ.ə.naɪz/

**核心定义**（Collins Dictionary）
1. **极度痛苦，苦恼**
   To suffer extreme pain or great anxiety.
2. **为...而极度忧虑**
   To think about something very carefully and with a lot of effort, especially because you cannot make a decision.

**典型例句**：
- She agonized over the decision for weeks.
- He agonized about whether to accept the job offer.
- Parents agonize over their children's education.
