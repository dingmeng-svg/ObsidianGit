## 词典查证
**音标**（Cambridge Dictionary）
英 /ˈaɪ.dəl.ɪŋ/  美 /ˈaɪ.dəl.ɪŋ/
(来自 idle /ˈaɪ.dəl/)

**核心定义**（Collins Dictionary）
1. **空转，闲置** (mechanical)
   When an engine is idling, it is running slowly while the vehicle is not moving. (COBUILD)
2. **无所事事，虚度光阴** (figurative)
   Spending time doing nothing useful; being inactive or unemployed.
3. **idle** v. to run at low speed (engine); to pass time without working.

**典型例句**：
- The car was idling at the curb while he waited for his passenger.
- Workers were idling outside the factory during the strike.
- Stop idling and get back to work!
- The engine was idling smoothly in the cold morning.

**原文补充**（阅读笔记）

**音标**：/ˈaɪ.dəl.ɪŋ/ (UK & US)

**释义**：闲置不工作；（引擎）空转

**典型用法**：idling 既指机械空转（引擎低速运转），也比喻人无所事事/虚度光阴

## 强对话链接
**近义词网络**（Thesaurus）

- **[[idling]]** → idling away, loafing, lounging, dawdling (human)
- **[[idling]]** → running idle, ticking over, stalling (mechanical)
- **[[idle]]** → inactive, unemployed, dormant, slack
- **[[loaf]]** → idle, laze, lounge, slack off

**语义关系**：
- idling (mechanical) vs idling (human)：机械=引擎空转（中性），人=虚度光阴（负面）
- idling vs loafing：idling 可中性或负面，loafing 纯负面（懒散）
- idling vs ticking over：ticking over 常用于引擎平稳空转（英式口语）
- idle vs lazy：idle 可临时状态，lazy 是性格特质

**典型搭配**：
- idling engine（空转引擎）
- idling car（怠速停车）
- idling away time（虚度时光）
- idling workers（闲置工人）
- stop idling（别闲着）
