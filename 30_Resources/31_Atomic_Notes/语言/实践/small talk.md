## 词典查证
**音标**（Cambridge Dictionary）
英 /smɔːl tɔːk/  美 /smɔːl tɑːk/

**核心定义**（Collins Dictionary）
1. **闲聊**
   Light conversation about unimportant matters
2. **社交谈话**
   Casual conversation to establish rapport

**典型例句**：
- Make small talk at parties.
- They engaged in small talk.
- Small talk filled the awkward silence.
