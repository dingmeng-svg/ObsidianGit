## 词典查证
**音标**（Cambridge Dictionary）
英 /lɪlt/ 美 /lɪlt/

**核心定义**（Collins Dictionary）
1. **轻柔的节奏**
   A gentle rising and falling of the voice
2. **愉悦的语调**
   A cheerful way of speaking

**典型例句**：
- She spoke with a lilt in her voice.
- The lilt of the music was infectious.
