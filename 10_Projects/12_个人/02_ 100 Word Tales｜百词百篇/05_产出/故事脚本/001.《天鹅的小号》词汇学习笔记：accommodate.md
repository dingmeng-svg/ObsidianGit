## 故事概述

### 英文版（English Version）

Once upon a time, there was a little swan named Louis. He lived in a place called Red Rock Lake. Louis was different from other swans because he could not make a sound! Can you imagine? A swan that could not honk!

Louis' mom and dad were worried, but Louis did not give up. He met a little boy named Sam and they became good friends. Sam taught Louis how to read and write. He even took Louis to school!

But when Louis went back to the other swans, he found that what he learned was not useful. He also liked a pretty swan named Serena, but he did not know how to tell her.

To help Louis, his dad took a risk and stole a little trumpet. Louis decided to learn to play the trumpet and use music to show his feelings.

To pay back his dad, Louis went on an exciting trip. He played the trumpet at a summer camp, on a swan boat in Boston, and even became a star in a zoo! Louis not only learned to play the trumpet, but also made many friends and earned money.

In the end, Louis' beautiful music touched Serena's heart. They went back to Red Rock Lake together and had a happy family.

## 核心词汇

### accommodate

| 项目 | 内容 |
|------|------|
| 音标 | UK /əˈkɒm.ə.deɪt/；US /əˈkɑː.mə.deɪt/ |
| 词性 | verb [T] |
| 中文释义 | 1. 容纳；2. 帮助，满足需求 |

### 词义详解

1. **容纳，提供空间**（To have room for someone or something; hold）
   - 例：The auditorium can accommodate 500 people.
   - 中文：这个大礼堂可以容纳500人。

2. **帮助，满足需求**（To do a favor or service for someone）
   - 例：I wanted to pick up the cake the next day, but the baker could not accommodate me.
   - 中文：我想第二天取蛋糕，但是蛋糕师不能满足我的要求。

### 原文例句

> When the Swan Boat docked and the passengers got off, long lines of people were waiting to get aboard for the next ride. Business was booming. Another boat was being made ready, to **accommodate** the crowds. Everyone wanted to ride the Swan Boats behind a real live swan playing a trumpet. It was the biggest happening in Boston in a long time.
>
> —— E. B. White, *The Trumpet of the Swan*

### 词义应用场景

#### 场景1：安排会议室（Arranging a meeting room）

> Our conference room can accommodate up to 20 people comfortably.

**对话练习：**

Employee A: Manager Li, we need a meeting room that can hold 15 people for next Tuesday.

Manager Li: No problem, our large conference room can easily accommodate 20 people. I'll book it for you right now.

Employee A: That's great, thank you. We might also need a projector and a whiteboard.

Manager Li: Don't worry, the room is already equipped with these facilities. It can fully accommodate your needs.

#### 场景2：处理客户特殊要求（Handling special requests）

> We always try our best to accommodate our clients' special requests.

**对话练习：**

Customer: Hello, I have a severe peanut allergy. I was wondering if your restaurant can prepare dishes without peanuts for me?

Server: Of course, we always strive to accommodate our customers' special needs. I will inform the kitchen immediately to ensure your meal is completely peanut-free.

Customer: Thank you so much. I was really worried about this issue.

Server: Please don't worry. We will handle your request with the utmost care. Your health and dining experience are of the highest importance to us.

#### 场景3：调整工作计划配合同事（Adjusting work schedules）

> I'm willing to accommodate my schedule to help with the urgent project.

**对话练习：**

Manager Zhang: Wang, our new project has suddenly been moved up and we need to complete the first draft by this Friday. Can you adjust your schedule to help out?

Wang: I understand the urgency of the situation. Although I had planned other tasks, I'm willing to accommodate my schedule to assist with this pressing project.

Manager Zhang: Thank you so much. I know this might disrupt your original plans.

Wang: It's alright. Teamwork is all about accommodating each other. Let's work together, and I'm sure we can complete the task on time.

### 语言点分析

| 项目 | 说明 |
|------|------|
| **发音** | /əˈkɒm.ə.deɪt/ — 注意重音在第二个音节 |
| **词形变化** | accommodates, accommodated, accommodating |
| **同义词对比** | 含义1: hold, fit, house；含义2: oblige, assist, cater to |
| **搭配用法** | accommodate + 数量（The hotel can accommodate up to 200 guests.）；accommodate + to（We need to accommodate to the new schedule.）；accommodate + with（Can you accommodate me with an early check-in?） |

### 词汇教学建议

**词义介绍：**

"accommodate"是一个神奇的词，它有两个主要的含义：
- 第一个意思是"容纳"或"安置"，就像一个大口袋可以装下很多很多人
- 第二个意思是"帮助"或"满足需求"，就像有人请求你帮忙，而你尽力去满足他们的需求

**互动游戏：**

1. "容纳大挑战"：数数看你的房间可以容纳（accommodate）多少人？
2. "帮助小天使"游戏：轮流扮演"帮助小天使"，尽量满足（accommodate）其他小朋友的请求。

**造句练习：**
- 我们的教室可以容纳（accommodate）____个学生。
- 妈妈总是能满足（accommodate）我对新玩具的要求。

**课后小任务：**
找一找家里最能"accommodate"东西的地方（比如衣柜、书架等），下次上课时和大家分享。

## 故事寓意

### 英文版（This story tells us）

1. Everyone is special. Don't be sad if you are different from others.
2. When you have a problem, don't be afraid. Try to find a way to solve it.
3. Good friends can be anyone, even animals of different kinds.
4. Believe in your dreams. One day, they will come true.
5. Be thankful and use what you can do to help others.

### 中文版（这个故事告诉我们）

1. 每个人都是特别的，不要因为和别人不一样就难过。
2. 遇到困难不要怕，要想办法解决。
3. 好朋友可以是任何人，甚至是不同种类的动物。
4. 坚持自己的梦想，总有一天会实现的。
5. 要懂得感恩，用自己的本领帮助别人。

## 项目背景

本篇是 "100 Word Tales｜百字百篇" 项目的第一篇。该项目以 American Heritage 出版社的《The 100 Words Every Fourth Grader Should Know》为基础，选取书中的100个核心词汇，每个词配以一个源自儿童文学的例句，通过讲述一个完整的故事来帮助孩子们在语境中理解和记忆单词。

本书中的例句大多出自经典儿童文学，如 E. B. White 的《天鹅的小号》（*The Trumpet of the Swan*），以及四年级学生能够读懂的书籍。
