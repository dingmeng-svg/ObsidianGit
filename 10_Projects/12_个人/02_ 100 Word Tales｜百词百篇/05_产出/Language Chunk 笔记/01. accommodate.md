## 词典查证
**音标**（Cambridge Dictionary）
英 /əˈkɒm.ə.deɪt/ 美 /əˈkɑː.mə.deɪt/

**核心定义**（Collins Dictionary）
1. 提供住宿：To provide someone with a place to stay
2. 容纳：To have enough space for
3. 适应：To change your behavior to deal with a new situation

**例句**（Collins）
1. The hotel can accommodate up to 500 guests.
2. We need to accommodate the new schedule.
3. He accommodated himself to the new environment.

**原文补充**（100 Words）

**音标**：(ə kŏm′ə dāt′)

**定义**：
1. To have room for someone or something; hold
   例：The auditorium can accommodate 500 people.
2. To do a favor or service for someone
   例：I wanted to pick up the cake the next day, but the baker could not accommodate me.

**文学例句**：
When the Swan Boat docked and the passengers got off, long lines of people were waiting to get aboard for the next ride. Business was booming. Another boat was being made ready, to accommodate the crowds. Everyone wanted to ride the Swan Boats behind a real live swan playing a trumpet. It was the biggest happening in Boston in a long time.
— E. B. White, *The Trumpet of the Swan*
