## 提纯

## 词典查证
**音标**（Cambridge Dictionary）
- 英式：/ˈfʌŋk.ʃᵊn/
- 美式：/ˈfʌŋk.ʃᵊn/

**核心定义**（Collins Dictionary）
1. **[noun] 功能、作用**： the purpose or job of something  
   例：The main function of the heart is to pump blood.（心脏的主要功能是泵血。）
   
2. **[noun] 职责**： an official duty or responsibility  
   例：Her function in the organization is to manage finances.（她在组织中的职责是管理财务。）
   
3. **[verb] 运作、起作用**： to work or operate in the correct or intended way  
   例：The system functions smoothly.（系统运作顺畅。）

**原文补充**（100 Words）

**音标**：(fŭngk′shən) noun & verb

**定义**：
1. Noun: The proper activity of a person or thing; a purpose or use
   例：The function of a thermometer is to measure temperature.
2. Noun: A formal social gathering or official ceremony, like a wedding.
3. Verb: To have or perform a function; serve
   例：This post functions as a support. My printer isn't functioning right.

**文学例句**：
Without warning, coming as a complete and unexpected shock, she felt a pressure she had never imagined, as though she were being completely flattened out by an enormous steamroller. . . . She tried to gasp, but a paper doll can't gasp. She thought she was trying to think, but her flattened‑out mind was as unable to function as her lungs.
— Madeleine L'Engle, *A Wrinkle in Time*

## 注意力锚
function 的增量在于它同时跨越名词和动词，且"名词=功能，动词=运作"的语义一致性是高频词中值得关注的模式

## 类型
定义/语域

## 正文
function 作名词表示"功能、作用、职责"，作动词表示"运作、运行、起作用"。常见搭配：perform a function, serve a function, function properly/well。语域中性，广泛用于技术、生物、社会等领域。
