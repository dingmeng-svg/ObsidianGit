## 提纯

## 词典查证
**音标**（Cambridge Dictionary）
- 英式：/ʌnˈskruː.pjə.ləs/
- 美式：/ʌnˈskruː.pjə.ləs/

**核心定义**（Collins Dictionary）
1. [ADJ] Someone who is unscrupulous uses immoral methods to get what they want. 无耻的；不择手段的
   - 例句：Unscrupulous businessmen exploited the workers for profit. 无良商人剥削工人牟利。
2. [ADJ] Having or showing no moral principles; not honest or fair. 缺乏道德原则的
   - 例句：He was willing to use unscrupulous tactics to win the election. 他愿意使用不择手段的策略赢得选举。

**原文补充**（100 Words）

**音标**：*(ŭn skr ′pyə ləs) adjective*

**定义**：
Not honest or trustworthy:
例：We watched a movie about an unscrupulous businessman who cheated his partners.

**文学例句**：
In ancient, more honest times, a man could only earn the title of scholar after years of long study and passing three exams, each one harder than the last one. However, owning even the lowest scholarly title—whether earned or bought—gave the holder and his clan certain advantages. For one thing, unscrupulous people like the Phoenixes could use the title to get out of certain kinds of taxes; but more importantly, it made it easier for the Phoenixes to see the government officials who were "brother" scholars.
— Laurence Yep, *The Serpent's Children*

## 注意力锚
unscrupulous 源自 scruple（顾忌/良心不安），un-（否定）+ scrupulous（有顾忌的）= "毫无顾忌的"，这一构词分析揭示其核心语义"不道德/不择手段"

## 类型
形容词 / 贬义 / 正式

## 正文
unscrupulous 描述为了达成目的不惜采用不道德手段的人或行为，缺乏良心和道德原则。常见搭配：unscrupulous businessman（无良商人）、unscrupulous tactics（不择手段的策略）、unscrupulous methods。与 dishonest 的区分：dishonest 更侧重"不诚实"，unscrupulous 更侧重"不择手段/毫无顾忌"。与 ruthless 的区分：ruthless 更侧重"冷酷无情"，unscrupulous 更侧重"不道德/无视规则"。语域正式偏贬义，CEFR 约在 C1-C2 区间。
