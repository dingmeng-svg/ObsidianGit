## 词典查证
**音标**（Cambridge Dictionary）
英 /kəmˈplek.ʃən/ 美 /kəmˈplek.ʃən/

**核心定义**（Collins Dictionary）
1. 肤色：The natural color of the skin on your face
2. 性质：The general character of something

**例句**（Collins）
1. She has a fair complexion.
2. The situation has changed in complexion.

**原文补充**（100 Words）

**音标**：(kəm plĕk′shən)

**定义**：
1. The natural color of a person's skin, especially that of the face
   例：Which makeup is best for my complexion?

**文学例句**：
Darnell looked a lot like his sister, except that he was just a little lighter in complexion.
— Walter Dean Myers, *Darnell Rock Reporting*
