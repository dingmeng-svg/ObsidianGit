## 提纯
transform — 转变，改造，使发生根本性的形态或性质变化

## 词典查证
**音标**（Cambridge Dictionary）
- 英式：/trænsˈfɔːm/
- 美式：/trænsˈfɔːrm/

**核心定义**（Collins Dictionary）
1. [T] To transform something means to change it completely. 转变；改造
   - 例句：The experience transformed her into a more confident person. 这段经历使她变成了一个更自信的人。
2. [T] To change the form, appearance, or nature of something. 改变形态、外观或性质
   - 例句：The new management transformed the company's fortunes. 新管理层改变了公司的命运。

**原文补充**（100 Words）

**音标**：*(trăns fôrm′) verb*

**定义**：
1. To change the form or appearance of someone or something by a large amount:
   例：The snow completely transformed the landscape.
2. To change the nature, function, or condition of something; convert:
   例：A steam engine transforms heat into power.

**文学例句**：
An hour later, Jessie and Megan had transformed the little wooden puppet theater in Megan's basement into the hottest new lemonade stand on the block.
— Jacqueline Davies, *The Lemonade War*

## 注意力锚
transform 与 change、convert、alter 构成"改变"语义场，但 transform 强调"根本性的/彻底的转变"，trans- 前缀（跨越）+ form（形态）= "跨越原有形态"，这一构词分析有助于理解其强度

## 类型
动词 / 变化 / 中性

## 正文
transform 指使某物在形态、性质或外观上发生彻底的转变。与 change 的关键区分：change 更泛化，transform 更强调"彻底/根本性的变化"。与 convert 的区分：convert 更侧重"转换用途/形式"，transform 更侧重"质的改变"。常见搭配：transform sth into sth, transform one's life, transform the company。被动语态也很常见：be transformed by。语域中性，CEFR 约在 B2 区间。

## 强对话链接
**[[transform]]** → change, convert, metamorphose, transmute
- change → become different
- convert → change from one form to another
- metamorphose → change completely (biological/literary)
- transmute → change in nature/substance
- [[change]] → transform 的近义，change 更泛化，transform 更强调根本性变化
- [[convert]] → transform 的近义，convert 更侧重转换形式，transform 更侧重质的改变
