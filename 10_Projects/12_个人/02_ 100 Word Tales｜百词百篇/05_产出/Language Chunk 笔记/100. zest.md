## 提纯
zest — 热情，风味，对生活的热情兴奋或食物的清新风味

## 词典查证
**音标**（Cambridge Dictionary）
- 英式：/zest/
- 美式：/zest/

**核心定义**（Collins Dictionary）
1. **[uncountable] 热情、兴致**：enthusiasm, eagerness, energy, and excitement  
   例：She had a zest for life that inspired everyone around her.（她对生活的热情激励着身边的每个人。）
   
2. **[uncountable] 风味、滋味**：the quality of being pleasant or interesting  
   例：The lemon zest added flavor to the cake.（柠檬皮为蛋糕增添了风味。）
   
3. **[countable] 柑橘皮**：the outer skin of a citrus fruit used for flavoring  
   例：Grate some orange zest into the mixture.（将一些橙皮磨碎加入混合物中。）

**原文补充**（100 Words）

**音标**：*(zĕst) noun*

**定义**：
1. Flavor or interest:
   例：Spices give zest to simple foods.
2. Great enjoyment; relish:
   例：Kevin ate his meal with zest.

**文学例句**：
The Queen's class, left behind in school while the others scattered to green lanes and leafy wood cuts and meadow byways, looked wistfully out of the windows and discovered that Latin verbs and French exercises had somehow lost the tang and zest they had possessed in the crisp winter months.
— L. M. Montgomery, *Anne of Green Gables*

## 注意力锚
zest 具有双重语义：①抽象层面"热情/兴致" ②具体层面"柑橘皮的风味"，两义共享"清新/活力"的核心特质，这一从具体到抽象的隐喻映射值得关注

## 类型
名词 / 多义 / 中性-褒义

## 正文
zest 有两个主要义项：①对生活的热情、兴致、活力（zest for life, zest for learning）；②柑橘类水果的果皮风味（lemon zest, orange zest，烹饪术语）。两个义项共享"清新/活力"的核心语义。与 enthusiasm 的区分：enthusiasm 更侧重"热情/热忱"，zest 更侧重"活力/兴致勃勃"。与 passion 的区分：passion 更侧重"强烈情感"，zest 更侧重"愉悦的兴致"。语域中性偏褒义，CEFR 约在 C1 区间。典型搭配：zest for life, add zest to, with zest。
