## 词典查证
**音标**（Cambridge Dictionary）
英 /krɪndʒ/ 美 /krɪndʒ/

**核心定义**（Collins Dictionary）
1. 退缩：To move back in fear or disgust
2. 尴尬：To feel embarrassed

**例句**（Collins）
1. He cringed at the sight of blood.
2. She cringed when she heard the bad joke.

**原文补充**（100 Words）

**音标**：(krĭnj)

**定义**：
1. To shiver or move your body suddenly out of fear, pain, embarrassment, or disgust
   例：Kayla cringed when the dog growled at her.

**文学例句**：
Maniac uncrumpled the page, flattened it out as best he could. How could he return the book to Amanda in this condition? He couldn't. But he had to. It was hers. Judging from that morning, she was pretty finicky about her books. Maniac cringed at both prospects.
— Jerry Spinelli, *Maniac Magee*
