## 提纯
momentary 指短暂的、瞬间的，核心语义是"仅持续一瞬的"。

## 词典查证
**音标**（Cambridge Dictionary）
- UK: /ˈməʊməntri/
- US: /ˈmoʊmənteri/

**核心定义**（Collins Dictionary）
1. **adj. 短暂的，瞬间的** — Something momentary lasts for a very short time. 例：There was a momentary silence before the crowd erupted.
2. **adj. 转瞬即逝的** — Momentary feelings or actions pass quickly. 例：She felt a momentary pang of regret.

**原文补充**（100 Words）

**音标**：*(mō′mən tĕr′ē) adjective*

**定义**：
1. Lasting only for a short period of time:  
   *The sun shone briefly during a momentary break in the clouds.*

**文学例句**：
> Like some animal that doesn't know how to climb a tree, never has known how to climb a tree, but thinks it will just try anyway, the bear went up the trunk by main force; and he didn't so much climb down as fall, interrupting his plunge with five or six desperate momentary pauses.  
> — Randall Jarrell, *The Animal Family*

## 注意力锚
momentary 与 momentous 形似义反——前者"短暂的"，后者"重大的"，是易混淆词对，需特别注意区分。

## 类型
定义/语域

## 正文
momentary 是形容词，指短暂的、瞬间的、转瞬即逝的，强调持续时间极短。常描述注意力、犹豫、恐惧等心理状态或物理动作。与 brief 相比更强调"一瞬"的极短时间感。语域通用/正式。
