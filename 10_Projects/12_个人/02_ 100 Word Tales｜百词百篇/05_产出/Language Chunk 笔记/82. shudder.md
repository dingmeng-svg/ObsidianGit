## 提纯
shudder — 战栗，发抖，因恐惧、厌恶或寒冷而产生的剧烈颤抖

## 词典查证
**音标**（Cambridge Dictionary）
- 英式：/ˈʃʌd.ər/
- 美式：/ˈʃʌd.ɚ/

**核心定义**（Collins Dictionary）
1. [I] If you shudder, you shake slightly because you feel cold, frightened, or disgusted. 战栗；发抖
   - 例句：She shuddered with horror at the thought of what she had seen. 想起所见之物，她恐惧地战栗。
2. [C] A shudder is a momentary shaking movement of the body. 战栗；颤抖
   - 例句：A shudder ran through her body. 一阵战栗传遍她的全身。

**原文补充**（100 Words）

**音标**：*(shŭd′ər) verb*

**定义**：
To tremble or shiver suddenly, especially from fear or cold:
例：We all shuddered as we went into the dark cave.

**文学例句**：
The judge came in and sat down and we did the same. Skyla and Clive on one side and Gram, Owen, and I on the other. Even though the judge looked friendly with smile wrinkles around her eyes like Fabiola's, as soon as I saw that black robe, I shuddered inside. It meant the law, that whatever the judge said would have to be carried out.
— Pam Muñoz Ryan, *Becoming Naomi León*

## 注意力锚
shudder 与 shiver、tremble 构成"颤抖"语义场的精细区分：shudder 强调"因恐惧/厌恶而剧烈颤抖"，shiver 侧重"因寒冷而发抖"，tremble 侧重"因紧张/激动而轻微颤抖"

## 类型
动词/名词 / 身体反应 / 中性-文学

## 正文
shudder 描述因恐惧、厌恶或寒冷而引起的剧烈颤抖，既可作动词也可作名词。与 shiver 的关键区分：shiver 主要因寒冷引起，shudder 主要因恐惧或厌恶引起。与 tremble 的区分：tremble 更轻微更持续，shudder 更剧烈更短暂。常见搭配：shudder at the thought, shudder with fear/horror, a shudder ran through sb。语域中性偏文学，CEFR 约在 C1 区间。

## 强对话链接
**[[shudder]]** → shiver, tremble, quake, cringe
- shiver → shaking from cold
- tremble → slight, continuous shaking
- quake → violent shaking (fear/cold)
- cringe → shrink back in disgust
- [[shiver]] → shudder 的近义，shiver 侧重寒冷引起的发抖，shudder 侧重恐惧/厌恶引起的战栗
- [[tremble]] → shudder 的近义，tremble 更轻微持续，shudder 更剧烈短暂
