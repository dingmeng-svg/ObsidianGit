## 提纯
quaver 指颤抖、发颤音，核心语义是"因紧张或情绪导致的震动或颤音"。

## 词典查证
**音标**（Cambridge Dictionary）
- UK: /ˈkweɪvə/
- US: /ˈkweɪvər/

**核心定义**（Collins Dictionary）
1. **v. 颤抖，发颤音** — If your voice quavers, it shakes because of emotion. 例：Her voice quavered with emotion as she spoke.
2. **n. 颤音；八分音符** — A quaver is a musical note (eighth note in US English). 例：The melody was played in quick quavers.

**原文补充**（100 Words）

**音标**：*(kwā′vər) verb*

**定义**：
1. To be unsteady in pitch or volume:  
   *His voice quavered as he spoke in front of the large crowd.*

**文学例句**：
> The Archmage Nemmerle, Warder of Roke, was an old man, older it was said than any man then living. His voice quavered like the bird's voice when he spoke, welcoming Ged kindly.  
> — Ursula K. Le Guin, *A Wizard of Earthsea*

## 注意力锚
quaver 的音乐义（八分音符）与动词义（颤抖）同源——都源于"波动、震动"的概念。英式英语中 quaver 是八分音符，美式英语中叫 eighth note。

定义/语域

## 正文
quaver 是动词和名词，指颤抖、发颤音，因紧张、恐惧或强烈情绪导致声音或身体震动。作动词指用颤抖的声音说话或身体微颤；作名词可指颤音或八分音符（音乐术语，英式用法）。与 tremble 相比更侧重声音的颤抖，与 shake 相比更细微。语域偏文学/音乐。
