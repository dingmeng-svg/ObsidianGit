## 提纯

## 词典查证
**音标**（Cambridge Dictionary）
- 英式：/jiːld/
- 美式：/jiːld/

**核心定义**（Collins Dictionary）
1. [I/T] If you yield to someone or something, you give in to them. 屈服；让步
   - 例句：The government refused to yield to terrorist demands. 政府拒绝向恐怖分子的要求屈服。
2. [T] If something yields a result, it produces it. 产出；产生
   - 例句：The experiment yielded surprising results. 实验得出了令人惊讶的结果。

**原文补充**（100 Words）

**音标**：*(yēld) verb & noun*

**定义**：
**Verb:**
1. To give or produce something:
   例：The fertile soil yielded a large crop. The improved process will yield better results.
2. To give something up; surrender:
   例：The soldiers yielded the fort to the attacking army.
3. To give in; submit:
   例：We yielded to their arguments.
4. To give way to physical pressure or force:
   例：The soft dough yields when pressed with a finger.

**Noun:**
An amount that is produced:
例：We hope to increase our yield of tomatoes this year.

**文学例句**：
"Very well, then," said the Badger firmly, rising to his feet. "Since you won't yield to persuasion, we'll try what force can do. I feared it would come to this all along."
— Kenneth Grahame, *The Wind in the Willows*

## 注意力锚
yield 的两个义项看似无关但共享"给予/让渡"的核心语义：①屈服 = 让渡立场/控制 ②产出 = 让渡产品/结果，这一深层语义联系有助于统一理解

## 类型
动词/名词 / 多义 / 中性

## 正文
yield 有两个主要义项：①屈服、让步（yield to pressure, yield to temptation）；②产出、提供结果（yield results, yield crops）。两个义项共享"给予/让渡"的核心语义。作名词时指"产量/收益"（high yield, crop yield）。与 surrender 的区分：surrender 更侧重"彻底投降"，yield 更侧重"让步/妥协"。与 produce 的区分：produce 更侧重"制造"，yield 更侧重"自然产出/提供结果"。语域中性，CEFR 约在 B2 区间。

## 强对话链接
**[[yield]]** → surrender (义项①), submit (义项①), produce (义项②), generate (义项②)
- surrender → give up completely
- submit → give in to authority
- produce → make, create
- generate → bring into existence
- [[surrender]] → yield 义项①的近义，surrender 更彻底，yield 更侧重让步妥协
- [[produce]] → yield 义项②的近义，produce 更侧重制造，yield 更侧重自然产出
