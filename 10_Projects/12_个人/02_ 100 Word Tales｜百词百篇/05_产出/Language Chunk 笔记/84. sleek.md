## 提纯
sleek — 光滑的，流线型的，形容物体表面光滑或设计时尚优雅

## 词典查证
**音标**（Cambridge Dictionary）
- 英式：/sliːk/
- 美式：/sliːk/

**核心定义**（Collins Dictionary）
1. [ADJ] Sleek hair or fur is smooth and shiny. 光滑有光泽的
   - 例句：Her hair was sleek and glossy. 她的头发光滑有光泽。
2. [ADJ] Sleek vehicles or objects have a smooth, graceful shape. 流线型的；时尚优雅的
   - 例句：The car has a sleek, aerodynamic design. 这辆车拥有流线型的空气动力学设计。

**原文补充**（100 Words）

**音标**：*(slēk) adjective*

**定义**：
Very smooth and glossy:
例：The horse has a sleek coat.

**文学例句**：
All day the sun beat hot on the house. All day it was full of the crawling sound that went up the wall and over the roof and down. All day grasshoppers' heads with bulging eyes, and grasshoppers' legs clutching, were thick along the bottom edge of the shut windows; all day they tried to walk up the sleek glass and fell back, while thousands more pushed up and tried and fell.
— Laura Ingalls Wilder, *On the Banks of Plum Creek*

## 注意力锚
sleek 与 smooth、glossy 构成"光滑"语义场，但 sleek 特指"光滑且时尚/优雅"，常用于现代产品设计、汽车、发型等语境，带有"现代感/高级感"

## 类型
形容词 / 描述性 / 中性-褒义

## 正文
sleek 描述表面光滑且有光泽，或设计时尚优雅、线条流畅。既可形容物体（sleek hair 光滑的头发, a sleek car 流线型跑车），也可形容动物（a sleek cat 毛色光亮的猫）。与 smooth 的区分：smooth 侧重"光滑无粗糙"，sleek 侧重"光滑且有光泽/时尚感"。与 glossy 的区分：glossy 更侧重"有光泽"，sleek 更侧重"光滑且优雅"。语域中性偏褒义，CEFR 约在 B2-C1 区间。

## 强对话链接
**[[sleek]]** → smooth, glossy, polished, streamlined
- smooth → even surface, no roughness
- glossy → shiny, reflective surface
- polished → made smooth and shiny
- streamlined → efficient, modern design
- [[smooth]] → sleek 的近义，smooth 更泛化，sleek 更强调光泽和时尚感
- [[glossy]] → sleek 的近义，glossy 更侧重光泽度，sleek 更侧重整体质感
