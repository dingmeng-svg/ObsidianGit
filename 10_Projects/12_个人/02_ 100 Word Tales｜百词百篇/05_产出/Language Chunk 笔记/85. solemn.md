## 提纯
solemn — 庄严的，严肃的，形容仪式、承诺或表情的庄重与认真

## 词典查证
**音标**（Cambridge Dictionary）
- 英式：/ˈsɒl.əm/
- 美式：/ˈsɑː.ləm/

**核心定义**（Collins Dictionary）
1. [ADJ] Someone or something that is solemn is very serious and not cheerful. 庄严的；严肃的
   - 例句：His face was solemn as he announced the news. 宣布消息时，他的神情庄严。
2. [ADJ] A solemn promise is one that is made with great seriousness. 庄重的；郑重的
   - 例句：They made a solemn vow never to reveal the secret. 他们庄严发誓永不泄露秘密。

**原文补充**（100 Words）

**音标**：*(sŏl′əm) adjective*

**定义**：
1. Very serious; grave:
   例：A funeral is a solemn occasion.
2. Made or taken after serious thought and with an understanding of possible consequences:
   例：Sophia made a solemn promise never to reveal the secret.

**文学例句**：
They were to be allowed to use the sailing boat by themselves. They were to be allowed to sail out from the little sheltered bay, and round the point, and down the lake to the island. They were to be allowed to land on the island, and to live there until it was time to pack up again and go home to town and school and lessons. The news was so good that it made them solemn. They ate their bread and marmalade in silence.
— Arthur Ransome, *Swallows and Amazons*

## 注意力锚
solemn 与 serious、grave 构成"严肃"语义场，但 solemn 特指"仪式感的庄重"，常用于宗教、法律、誓词等正式场合，带有"神圣/不可侵犯"的语域标记

## 类型
形容词 / 正式 / 仪式-法律

## 正文
solemn 描述庄重、严肃且带有仪式感的氛围或态度，常用于正式场合：宗教仪式（solemn ceremony）、法律承诺（solemn oath）、严肃表情（solemn expression）。与 serious 的区分：serious 更泛化，solemn 更强调庄重和仪式感。与 grave 的区分：grave 更侧重"严重性/重要性"，solemn 更侧重"庄重性/神圣性"。语域正式，CEFR 约在 B2-C1 区间。典型搭配：solemn promise, solemn occasion, solemn voice。

## 强对话链接
