## 词典查证
**音标**（Cambridge Dictionary）
- 英式：/ˈɡlɪm.əʳ/
- 美式：/ˈɡlɪm.ər/

**核心定义**（Collins Dictionary）
1. **[noun] 微弱闪烁的光**： a faint, unsteady light  
   例：A glimmer of light appeared through the curtains.（透过窗帘出现了一丝微光。）
   
2. **[noun] 微弱的迹象（隐喻）**： a small sign or trace of something  
   例：There was a glimmer of hope in her eyes.（她眼中闪烁着一丝希望。）
   
3. **[verb] 微弱地闪烁**： to shine with a faint, unsteady light  
   例：The candle glimmered in the darkness.（蜡烛在黑暗中微微闪烁。）

**原文补充**（100 Words）

**音标**：(glĭm′ər) noun & verb

**定义**：
1. Noun: A dim, unsteady light
   例：I could barely see the red glimmer of the coals.
2. Noun: A faint indication; a trace
   例：We had a glimmer of hope.
3. Verb: To shine with a dim, unsteady light
   例：A candle glimmered in the distant window.

**文学例句**：
They went on, down and down with the running water, Curdie getting more and more afraid it was leading them to some terrible gulf in the heart of the mountain. In one or two places he had to break away the rock to make room before even Irene could get through—at least without hurting herself. But at length they spied a glimmer of light, and in a minute more, they were almost blinded by the full sunlight into which they emerged.
— George MacDonald, *The Princess and the Goblin*

## 注意力锚
glimmer 的增量在于它常被隐喻化用于描述"微弱的希望/可能性"——从物理光到抽象希望的延伸是值得关注的语义扩展模式

## 类型
定义/语域

## 正文
glimmer 指微弱、不定地闪烁的光。常见搭配：glimmer of light/hope/hope, glimmer in the darkness。既可描述物理光（candle glimmer），也可隐喻希望或可能性（a glimmer of hope）。语域中性偏文学。
