## 提纯
foremost — 最前面的/最重要的，强调首要地位

## 词典查证
**音标**（Cambridge Dictionary）
- 英式：/ˈfɔː.məʊst/
- 美式：/ˈfɔːr.moʊst/

**核心定义**（Collins Dictionary）
1. **[adjective] 最重要的、首要的**： most important or most powerful  
   例：He is the foremost authority on this subject.（他是该领域最重要的权威。）
   
2. **[adjective] 最前面的**： first in a series or position  
   例：The foremost runners in the race were already visible.（比赛中最前面的跑步者已经可见。）
   
3. **[phrase] first and foremost**： most importantly; before anything else  
   例：First and foremost, we need to ensure safety.（首先，我们需要确保安全。）

**原文补充**（100 Words）

**音标**：(fôr′mōst′)

**定义**：
1. First in rank, position, or importance; chief
   例：Shakespeare is the foremost playwright in English literature.

**文学例句**：
Lynn planned to become either a rocket scientist or a famous writer. Though I knew nothing about animals, she said that when I grew up I would go to Africa to study them. I can't say that the idea of college was foremost on my mind; nevertheless, if Lynn was going, I would too.
— Cynthia Kadohata, *Kira-Kira*

## 注意力锚
foremost 的增量在于它同时具有空间义（最前面）和等级义（最重要），且固定搭配 first and foremost 是高频语块

## 类型
定义/语域

## 正文
foremost 表示"最前面的"或"最重要的"，既可用于空间位置（foremost rank），也可用于重要性排序（the foremost expert）。固定搭配 first and foremost 表示"首先、首要的是"。语域正式。
