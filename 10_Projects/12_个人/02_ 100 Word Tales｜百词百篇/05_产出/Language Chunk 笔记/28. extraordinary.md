## 提纯
extraordinary — 非凡的、异乎寻常的，超出常规的

## 词典查证
**音标**（Cambridge Dictionary）
- 英式：/ɪkˈstrɔː.dᵊn.ri/ 或 /ˌek.strəˈɔː.dᵊn.ri/
- 美式：/ɪkˈstrɔːr.dᵊn.er.i/ 或 /ˌek.strəˈɔːr.dᵊn.er.i/

**核心定义**（Collins Dictionary）
1. **[adjective] 非凡的、异乎寻常的**：very unusual, special, unexpected, or strange  
   例：She showed extraordinary courage in the face of danger.（她在危险面前表现出非凡的勇气。）
   
2. **[adjective] 特别的、临时的**：arranged for a special purpose and therefore outside the usual pattern  
   例：An extraordinary meeting was called to discuss the crisis.（召开了一次特别会议讨论危机。）
   
3. **[adjective] 极好的、惊人的**：very good or impressive  
   例：It was an extraordinary performance that left the audience spellbound.（这是一场让观众着迷的精彩表演。）

**原文补充**（100 Words）

**音标**：(ĭk strôr′dn ĕr′ē or ĕk′strə ôr′dn ĕr′ē)

**定义**：
1. Very unusual; remarkable
   例：Landing on the moon was an extraordinary accomplishment.

**文学例句**：
Everybody in Billings bought a copy of the paper and read all about the extraordinary event. It was talked about all over town. Some people believed it; others said it never could have happened. They said the store owner had just invented it to get some publicity for his store. But the clerks in the store agreed that it had really happened. They pointed to the drops of blood on the floor.
— E. B. White, *The Trumpet of the Swan*

## 注意力锚
extraordinary 的构词法（extra + ordinary）本身就是语义的锚点——"超出寻常"，这种透明构词使它比 remarkable 等更易理解

## 类型
定义/语域

## 正文
extraordinary 表示远超寻常的、特别的、令人惊叹的。可用于描述人（extraordinary talent）、事件（extraordinary circumstances）、成就（extraordinary achievement）等。语域中性，既可用于正式场合也可用于日常表达。注意发音重音在 extra- 上。
