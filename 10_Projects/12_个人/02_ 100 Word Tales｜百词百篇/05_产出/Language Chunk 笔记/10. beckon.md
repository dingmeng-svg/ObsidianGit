## 词典查证
**音标**（Cambridge Dictionary）
英 /ˈbek.ən/ 美 /ˈbek.ən/

**核心定义**（Collins Dictionary）
1. 招手：To make a signal with your hand to tell someone to come
2. 吸引：To be attractive or inviting

**例句**（Collins）
1. She beckoned him to come closer.
2. The beautiful garden beckoned visitors.

**原文补充**（100 Words）

**音标**：(bĕk′ən)

**定义**：
1. To signal with a movement of the head or hand
   例：The coach beckoned me to come over to the bench.

**文学例句**：
The woman smiled and then beckoned Lissy to follow her to another room. Lissy followed the woman, and then we followed Lissy, like a parade.
— Grace Lin, *Dumpling Days*
