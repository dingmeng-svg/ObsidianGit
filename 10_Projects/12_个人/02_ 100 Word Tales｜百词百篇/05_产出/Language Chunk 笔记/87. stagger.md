## 提纯
stagger — 蹒跚，使震惊，既指走路不稳也指"使难以置信"

## 词典查证
**音标**（Cambridge Dictionary）
- 英式：/ˈstæɡ.ər/
- 美式：/ˈstæɡ.ɚ/

**核心定义**（Collins Dictionary）
1. [I] If you stagger, you walk very unsteadily, for example because you are ill or injured. 蹒跚；摇摇晃晃地走
   - 例句：The injured man staggered to his feet. 受伤的男人摇摇晃晃地站了起来。
2. [T] If something staggers you, it surprises or shocks you very much. 使震惊；使难以置信
   - 例句：The news of his death staggered everyone. 他去世的消息令所有人震惊。

**原文补充**（100 Words）

**音标**：*(stăg′ər) verb*

**定义**：
1. To walk unsteadily:
   例：The bear woke up and staggered out of its den.
2. To overwhelm someone, as with amazement:
   例：The size of the building staggered us.
3. To arrange something in alternating rows or time periods:
   例：The seats were staggered so that everyone could see the stage.

**文学例句**：
When his team was out in the field, the boys made Lewis play right field, because not many balls got hit out that way. But when one did, Lewis always dropped it, unless it hit him on the head. He would stagger back and forth as he tried to keep track of the ball that hung there, high over his head, but he always got dizzy and covered his face with his glove and screamed "No! No!" as the ball came down.
— John Bellairs, *The House with a Clock in its Walls*

## 注意力锚
stagger 具有双重语义：①身体层面的"蹒跚/摇摇晃晃" ②心理层面的"使震惊/使难以置信"，两义共享"失去平衡"的核心隐喻，这一多义性值得深度理解

## 类型
动词 / 多义 / 中性-戏剧性

## 正文
stagger 有两个主要义项：①走路不稳、摇摇晃晃（stagger under a heavy load, stagger into the room）；②使震惊、使难以置信（the news staggered me, staggering prices）。两个义项共享"失去平衡"的核心语义：物理上失去平衡 = 蹒跚；心理上失去平衡 = 震惊。作形容词用时（staggering）通常指"令人震惊的/惊人的"。语域中性偏戏剧性，CEFR 约在 B2-C1 区间。典型搭配：stagger backward, staggering amount, be staggered by。

## 强对话链接
**[[stagger]]** → stumble, lurch, reel, sway
- stumble → trip or lose balance
- lurch → move suddenly, unevenly
- reel → sway unsteadily
- sway → swing back and forth
- [[stumble]] → stagger 的近义，stumble 更侧重"绊倒"，stagger 更侧重"摇摇晃晃地走"
- [[astonish]] → stagger 的近义（心理义项），astonish 更侧重"惊讶"，stagger 更侧重"难以置信"
