## 词典查证
**音标**（Cambridge Dictionary）
英 /kəʊks/ 美 /koʊks/

**核心定义**（Collins Dictionary）
1. 哄劝：To persuade someone gently
2. 耐心说服：To get something by gentle persuasion

**例句**（Collins）
1. She coaxed him into taking the medicine.
2. He coaxed the cat out of the tree.

**原文补充**（100 Words）

**音标**：(kōks)

**定义**：
1. To persuade someone by gentle urging
   例：Rachel coaxed me into lending her my bicycle.
2. To get something by coaxing
   例：I coaxed a smile from the baby.

**文学例句**：
I remember the night perfectly, maybe even a little bit clearer than it actually was. Small hands slid over mine, over the jar, coaxing me back to the woods.
— Pam Conrad, *Stonewords: A Ghost Story*
