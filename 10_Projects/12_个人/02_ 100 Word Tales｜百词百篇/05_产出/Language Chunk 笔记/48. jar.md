## 提纯
jar 既是名词（罐子、广口瓶），也是动词（使不快、震动、冲突），核心语义是"突然的不和谐冲击"。

## 词典查证
**音标**（Cambridge Dictionary）
英 /dʒɑː/　美 /dʒɑːr/

**核心定义**（Collins Dictionary）
1. [a cylindrical glass or pottery container with a wide mouth] （广口瓶，罐子）
   例：She kept cookies in a large glass jar.
2. [to have an unpleasant or annoying effect] （使不快，刺耳）
   例：The bright colors jarred with the quiet atmosphere.
3. [to shake or vibrate] （震动，颠簸）
   例：The car jarred along the rough road.

**原文补充**（100 Words）

**音标**：*(jär)*

**定义**：
1. To bump or shake something strongly  
   例：*The explosion jarred buildings for miles around.*
2. To startle or upset someone  
   例：*We were jarred by the news that the famous baseball player had died.*

**文学例句**：
Thomas ran smack into a wall. His arms and hands hit first; then, his head and chest. The impact jarred him from head to foot.  
— Virginia Hamilton, *The House of Dies Drear*

## 注意力锚
jar 作动词时表示"刺耳、令人不快"的语义是增量价值——从物理容器到心理冲击的隐喻延伸。

## 类型
定义/语域

## 正文
jar 作名词指玻璃或陶制的广口容器，用于储存食物等；作动词表示产生不愉快的效果、发出刺耳声音，或使某人感到震惊不安。名词义常见于日常语境，动词义多用于描述感官或情感上的突兀冲突。
