## 提纯
lurch 指突然的倾斜或蹒跚移动，核心语义是"失控的突然偏移"。

## 词典查证
**音标**（Cambridge Dictionary）
英 /lɜːtʃ/　美 /lɜːrtʃ/

**核心定义**（Collins Dictionary）
1. [to make a sudden unsteady movement] （突然倾斜，蹒跚）
   例：The bus lurched to a sudden stop.
2. [leave someone in the lurch] （在危难时抛弃某人）
   例：He left me in the lurch when I needed help most.

**原文补充**（100 Words）

**音标**：*(lûrch)*

**定义**：
To move suddenly and unsteadily  
例：*We all lurched forward when the car swerved.*

**文学例句**：
Yuki waved and waved as the bus lurched down the dusty road. She kept waving even when she knew her friends could no longer see her and they became small black dots in the sand. She watched the black barracks and the hospital and the watchtowers grow smaller and smaller, until soon they were only a splotch in the desert.  
— Yoshiko Uchida, *Journey to Topaz*

## 注意力锚
"leave someone in the lurch"（在危难时抛弃某人）是高频习语，lurch 的物理倾斜义与情感上的"陷入困境"形成隐喻关联。

## 类型
定义/语域

## 正文
lurch 作动词指突然不稳地倾斜或蹒跚移动，常描述车辆、船只或人失去平衡时的动作；作名词指突然的倾斜。习语 "leave someone in the lurch" 意为在某人需要帮助时弃之不顾，语域偏日常。
