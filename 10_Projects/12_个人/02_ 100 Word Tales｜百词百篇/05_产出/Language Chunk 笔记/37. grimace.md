## 提纯
grimace — 痛苦或厌恶的面部表情（名词）；做苦相（动词）

## 词典查证
**音标**（Cambridge Dictionary）
- 英式：/ɡrɪˈmeɪs/ 或 /ˈɡrɪm.ɪs/
- 美式：/ɡrɪˈmeɪs/ 或 /ˈɡrɪm.ɪs/

**核心定义**（Collins Dictionary）
1. **[noun] 痛苦或厌恶的面部表情**： a facial expression that shows disgust, pain, or disapproval  
   例：She made a grimace when she tasted the bitter medicine.（当她尝到苦药时，她做了一个苦相。）
   
2. **[verb] 做苦相**： to make a facial expression showing disgust, pain, or disapproval  
   例：He grimaced at the sight of the injury.（看到伤口他做了个苦相。）
   
3. **[verb] 因痛苦或厌恶而扭曲面部**： to contort the face in an expression of pain or disgust  
   例：The patient grimaced as the doctor examined the wound.（医生检查伤口时病人痛苦地扭曲着脸。）

**原文补充**（100 Words）

**音标**：(grĭm′ĭs) noun & verb

**定义**：
1. Noun: A twisting or contortion of the face, usually from disgust or pain
   例：He made a grimace when he smelled the rotting food.
2. Verb: To twist or contort the face, usually from disgust or pain
   例：She grimaced with pain as she pulled out the splinter.

**文学例句**：
Reuven stood on the platform waving, waving like crazy. He could not make himself smile. He tried, but each time his lips pressed together into a grimace.
— Kathryn Lasky, *Broken Song*

## 注意力锚
grimace 的增量在于它将抽象情感具象化为具体的面部动作——是"表情词汇"中值得积累的一员

## 类型
定义/语域

## 正文
grimace 指因痛苦、厌恶或不满而扭曲面部的表情。常见搭配：grimace of pain/disgust, make a grimace, grimace at。既可作名词也可作动词。语域中性。
