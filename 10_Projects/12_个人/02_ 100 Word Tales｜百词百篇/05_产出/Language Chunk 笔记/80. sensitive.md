## 提纯
sensitive — 敏感的，善解人意的，对细微变化或他人感受有高度觉察力

## 词典查证
**音标**（Cambridge Dictionary）
英 /ˈsensətɪv/　美 /ˈsensətɪv/

**核心定义**（Collins Dictionary）
1. [easily affected or harmed by something] （敏感的，易受影响的）
   例：Don't be so sensitive — I wasn't criticizing you.
2. [aware of and understanding other people's feelings] （善解人意的，体贴的）
   例：She's very sensitive to other people's needs.
3. [kept secret or guarded] （机密的，须谨慎处理的）
   例：sensitive information

**原文补充**（100 Words）

**音标**：*(sĕn′sĭ tĭv) adjective*

**定义**：
1. Aware of or having sympathy for the feelings of others:  
   *Because my teacher is sensitive to others' feelings, she doesn't allow any teasing in the classroom.*
2. Quick to have your feelings hurt; touchy:  
   *Brandon is too sensitive to criticism.*
3. Easily irritated:  
   *Babies have sensitive skin.*
4. Able to perceive something through the senses:  
   *Dogs' ears are sensitive to sounds that most humans can't hear.*

**文学例句**：
> A short, round boy of seven, he took little interest in troublesome things, preferring to remain on good terms with everyone. Yet he was always sensitive to others and now, shifting the handle of his lunch can from his right hand to his right wrist and his smudged notebook from his left hand to his left armpit, he stuffed his free hands into his pockets and attempted to make his face as moody as Stacey's and as cranky as mine.  
> — Mildred D. Taylor, *Roll of Thunder, Hear My Cry*

## 注意力锚
sensitive 具有多义性：①感官/仪器的"灵敏" ②情感的"敏感/善解人意" ③话题的"敏感/需谨慎处理"，三义共享"对细微刺激有反应"的核心语义，掌握这一核心可统一理解

## 类型
形容词 / 多义 / 中性

## 正文
sensitive 有三个主要义项：①感官或仪器对细微刺激有高度反应（sensitive skin, a sensitive instrument）；②情感上善解人意、能体察他人感受（a sensitive person, sensitive to others' needs）；③话题需要谨慎处理（a sensitive issue, politically sensitive）。三义共享核心语义"对细微刺激有反应"。与 sensible 的关键区分：sensitive = "敏感的"，sensible = "明智的/合理的"。语域中性，CEFR 约在 B1-B2 区间。

## 强对话链接
**[[sensitive]]** → perceptive, responsive, delicate, impressionable
- perceptive → quick to notice/understand
- responsive → reacts readily to stimuli
- delicate → easily affected/damaged
- impressionable → easily influenced
- [[sensible]] → 形近易混词，sensible = 明智的/合理的，sensitive = 敏感的
- [[receptive]] → sensitive 的近义，receptive 更侧重"开放接受"，sensitive 更侧重"敏锐感知"
