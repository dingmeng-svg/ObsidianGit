## 提纯
foliage — 植物的叶子的总称（集合名词），尤指树木的叶簇

## 词典查证
**音标**（Cambridge Dictionary）
- 英式：/ˈfəʊ.li.ɪdʒ/
- 美式：/ˈfoʊ.li.ɪdʒ/

**核心定义**（Collins Dictionary）
1. **[uncountable] 叶子总称**：the leaves of a plant, or leaves on plants generally  
   例：The dense foliage provided cool shade in summer.（茂密的叶子在夏天提供了凉爽的树荫。）
   
2. **[uncountable] 叶簇**：a cluster of leaves  
   例：The autumn foliage was breathtakingly beautiful.（秋天的叶簇美得令人惊叹。）

**原文补充**（100 Words）

**音标**：(fō′lē ĭj)

**定义**：
1. The leaves of trees or other plants
   例：Gardeners grow some plants for their flowers and others for their foliage.

**文学例句**：
Knowing I couldn't turn my dogs loose, I broke off enough of the wire to lead them. As I passed under the branches of the bur oak tree, I looked up into the dark foliage. I could see the bright eyes of the ghost coon. Everything that had happened on this terrible night was because of his very existence, but it wasn't his fault.
— Wilson Rawls, *Where the Red Fern Grows*

## 注意力锚
foliage 是不可数名词，指"叶子"的集合体而非单片叶子（leaf），这个集合性是它与 leaf 的核心区别

## 类型
定义/语域

## 正文
foliage 是集合名词，指植物（尤指树木）的叶子整体。常见搭配：dense/lush foliage, autumn foliage, green foliage。与 leaf（单片叶子）不同，foliage 强调叶子的整体视觉效果和密度。语域正式偏文学，常用于自然描写。
