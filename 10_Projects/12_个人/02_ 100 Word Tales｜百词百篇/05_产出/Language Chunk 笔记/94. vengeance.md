## 提纯
vengeance — 复仇，报复，对伤害或错误的惩罚性回应

## 词典查证
**音标**（Cambridge Dictionary）
- 英式：/ˈven.dʒəns/
- 美式：/ˈven.dʒəns/

**核心定义**（Collins Dictionary）
1. [U] Vengeance is revenge for something done to you. 复仇；报复
   - 例句：He swore vengeance on the people who had destroyed his family. 他发誓要报复那些毁了他家庭的人。
2. [U] Punishment inflicted in return for an injury or wrong. 惩罚性报复
   - 例句：She sought vengeance for the wrongs done to her. 她为所受的冤屈寻求报复。

**原文补充**（100 Words）

**音标**：*(vĕn′jəns) noun*

**定义**：
The act of trying to hurt someone who has hurt you; revenge:
例：The king vowed to take vengeance on the prince who had tried to overthrow him.

**文学例句**：
He thought of his sister Shriprinka, his mother and father, and he thought of the music, the song begun long ago in the shtetl, broken by blood and vengeance in the horror of a single night.
— Kathryn Lasky, *Broken Song*

## 注意力锚
vengeance 与 revenge、retaliation 构成"复仇"语义场，但 vengeance 更正式更强调"惩罚性/正义性"，常用于文学和正式语境，短语 with a vengeance 是高频习语

## 类型
名词 / 法律-文学 / 正式

## 正文
vengeance 指对伤害或错误的惩罚性报复，带有"正义/惩罚"的语义色彩。常见搭配：seek vengeance（寻求复仇）、with a vengeance（猛烈地/过度地，习语）。与 revenge 的区分：revenge 更泛化更常用，vengeance 更正式更强调"惩罚性"。与 retaliation 的区分：retaliation 更侧重"反击/回击"，vengeance 更侧重"惩罚性报复"。语域正式偏文学，CEFR 约在 C1-C2 区间。习语 with a vengeance 表示"猛烈地/过度地"（He came back with a vengeance）。
