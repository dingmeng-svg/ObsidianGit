## 提纯
interior — 内部的、内部的（形容词）；内部、室内（名词）

## 词典查证
**音标**（Cambridge Dictionary）
- UK: /ɪnˈtɪəriə/
- US: /ɪnˈtiriər/

**核心定义**（Collins Dictionary）
1. **adj. 内部的，里面的** — The interior of something is the inside part of it. 例：The interior of the house was beautifully decorated.
2. **n. 内部，室内** — The interior is the inside part of a building, vehicle, or container. 例：The car's interior is spacious and comfortable.
3. **n. 内陆，内地** — The interior refers to regions far from the coast or borders. 例：They traveled deep into the interior of the country.

**原文补充**（100 Words）

**音标**：*(ĭn tîr′ē ər)*

**定义**：
**Noun:** An inner part; the inside  
例：*The interior of the earth is extremely hot.*

**Adjective:** Having to do with or located on the inside; inner  
例：*This paint is for the interior walls of your house.*

**文学例句**：
He tiptoed timidly up the three wooden steps to the door, tapped lightly, and leaped back in fright, for the moment he knocked there was a terrible crash inside the wagon that sounded as if a whole set of dishes had been dropped from the ceiling onto a hard stone floor. At the same time the door flew open, and from the dark interior a hoarse voice inquired, "Have you ever heard a whole set of dishes dropped from the ceiling onto a hard stone floor?"  
— Norton Juster, *The Phantom Tollbooth*

## 注意力锚
interior 的增量在于它与 exterior 形成对立词对——这种空间对立关系是值得积累的词汇网络

## 类型
定义/语域

## 正文
interior 作形容词表示"内部的"，作名词表示"内部、室内"。常见搭配：interior design, interior decorator, interior of, the interior（内陆/内地）。与 exterior（外部）相对。语域中性。
