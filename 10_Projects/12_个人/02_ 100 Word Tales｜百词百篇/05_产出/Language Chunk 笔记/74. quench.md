## 提纯
quench 指解渴、扑灭、压制，核心语义是"满足、消除强烈的渴望或状态"。

## 词典查证
**音标**（Cambridge Dictionary）
- UK: /kwentʃ/
- US: /kwentʃ/

**核心定义**（Collins Dictionary）
1. **v. 解渴** — To quench means to satisfy your thirst by drinking. 例：The cold water quenched his thirst after the long run.
2. **v. 扑灭，熄灭** — To quench also means to put out a fire or flame. 例：Firefighters quenched the flames before they spread.
3. **v. 满足，压制** — Quench can mean to satisfy a desire or put an end to something. 例：Nothing could quench his thirst for adventure.

**原文补充**（100 Words）

**音标**：*(kwĕnch) verb*

**定义**：
1. To satisfy a thirst by drinking something:  
   *I quenched my thirst with a glass of cold water.*
2. To put out a fire; extinguish:  
   *We quenched the campfire with a bucket of water.*

**文学例句**：
> That night they ate their very last scraps and crumbs of food; and next morning when they woke the first thing they noticed was that they were still gnawingly hungry, and the next thing was that it was raining and that here and there the drip of it was dropping heavily on the forest floor. That only reminded them that they were also parchingly thirsty, without doing anything to relieve them: you cannot quench a terrible thirst by standing under giant oaks and waiting for a chance drip to fall on your tongue.  
> — J. R. R. Tolkien, *The Hobbit*

## 注意力锚
quench 的隐喻用法（quench one's thirst for knowledge, quench a rebellion）是增量价值——从物理的"解渴/灭火"到抽象的"满足渴望/镇压叛乱"。

## 正文
quench 是动词，基本义指解渴、灭火，引申义指满足渴望、镇压或压制。常见搭配：quench one's thirst（解渴）、quench a fire（灭火）、quench one's desire（满足欲望）。与 satisfy 相比更强烈，暗示强烈需求的彻底满足。语域通用/文学。
