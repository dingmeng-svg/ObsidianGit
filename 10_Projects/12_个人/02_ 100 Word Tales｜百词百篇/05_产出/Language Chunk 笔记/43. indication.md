## 提纯
indication — 指示、迹象，表明某事物存在或可能发生的信号

## 词典查证
**音标**（Cambridge Dictionary）
- 英式：/ˌɪn.dɪˈkeɪ.ʃᵊn/
- 美式：/ˌɪn.dəˈkeɪ.ʃᵊn/

**核心定义**（Collins Dictionary）
1. **[noun, countable/uncountable] 迹象、征兆**： a sign or suggestion that something exists or is happening  
   例：There was no indication that he would resign.（没有任何迹象表明他会辞职。）
   
2. **[noun, countable] 指示、说明**： something that shows what something is like  
   例：The price is an indication of quality.（价格是质量的指示。）
   
3. **[noun, uncountable] 指向、标示**： the action of pointing or showing something  
   例：The indication of the direction was unclear.（方向的标示不清楚。）

**原文补充**（100 Words）

**音标**：*(ĭn′dĭ kā′shən)*

**定义**：
Something that indicates; a sign  
例：*A wrinkled forehead may be an indication of worry or deep thought.*

**文学例句**：
They found a wide gateway open and passed through it into a paved courtyard. And it was here that they had their first indication that there was something odd about this island. In the middle of the courtyard stood a pump, and beneath the pump a bucket. There was nothing odd about that. But the pump handle was moving up and down, though there seemed to be no one moving it.  
— C. S. Lewis, *The Voyage of the Dawn Treader*

## 注意力锚
indication 的增量在于"证据性"——它是推断或预测的依据，常与 give/show/provide 搭配

## 类型
定义/语域

## 正文
indication 表示某事物存在、发生或可能发生的迹象或信号。常见搭配：give an indication of, early indication, clear indication, indication that...。语域中性。
