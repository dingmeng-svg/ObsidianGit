## 词典查证
**音标**（Cambridge Dictionary）
英 /ˈder.ə.lɪkt/ 美 /ˈder.ə.lɪkt/

**核心定义**（Collins Dictionary）
1. 废弃的：No longer used or cared for
2. 玩忽职守的：Failing to do your duty

**例句**（Collins）
1. The derelict building was demolished.
2. He was accused of being derelict in his duties.

**原文补充**（100 Words）

**音标**：(dĕr′ə lĭkt′)

**定义**：
1. Deserted by an owner; abandoned
   例：The derelict garage was finally torn down.
2. Neglectful
   例：A good police officer is never derelict in his or her duty.

**文学例句**：
She started crying. She said we should never have left Random Road. We should never have come to this stinking derelict place.
— David Almond, *Skellig*
