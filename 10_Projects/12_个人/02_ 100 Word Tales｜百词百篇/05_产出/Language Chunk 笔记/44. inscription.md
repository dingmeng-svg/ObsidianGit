## 提纯
inscription — 铭文、题词，刻在或写在某物上的文字

## 词典查证
**音标**（Cambridge Dictionary）
- 英式：/ɪnˈskrɪp.ʃᵊn/
- 美式：/ɪnˈskrɪp.ʃᵊn/

**核心定义**（Collins Dictionary）
1. **[noun, countable] 铭文、题词**： words that are written or cut on something  
   例：There was an inscription on the monument honoring the soldiers.（纪念碑上刻有纪念士兵的铭文。）
   
2. **[noun, countable] 刻字**： a message written on or in something  
   例：The inscription inside the ring read "Forever Yours".（戒指内侧的刻字写着"永远属于你"。）
   
3. **[noun, uncountable] 刻写行为**： the act of inscribing something  
   例：The inscription of names on the wall took months.（在墙上刻写名字花了几个月时间。）

**原文补充**（100 Words）

**音标**：*(ĭn skrĭp′shən)*

**定义**：
1. The act of writing or carving on a surface  
   例：*The inscription of the message must have been done with a chisel.*
2. Something that is written or carved on a surface  
   例：*An interesting inscription is on the gate.*

**文学例句**：
That's when I realized where I was. The mossy rocks I'd noticed before were tombstones. Most were so old they blended into the trees and bushes, their inscriptions worn and covered with lichen.  
— Mary Downing Hahn, *Deep and Dark and Dangerous*

## 注意力锚
inscription 的增量在于"永久性+纪念性"——与简单的 writing 不同，inscription 常刻在纪念碑、牌匾、首饰等物体上，带有纪念意义

## 类型
定义/语域

## 正文
inscription 指刻写或镌刻在物体上的文字，如纪念碑、墓碑、牌匾、硬币、首饰上的铭文或题词。常见搭配：inscription on, Latin inscription, dedication inscription。语域正式。
