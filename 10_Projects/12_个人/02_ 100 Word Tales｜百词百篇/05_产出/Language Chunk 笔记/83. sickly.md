## 提纯
sickly — 多病的，病态的，形容经常生病或看起来不健康的人/物

## 词典查证
**音标**（Cambridge Dictionary）
- 英式：/ˈsɪk.li/
- 美式：/ˈsɪk.li/

**核心定义**（Collins Dictionary）
1. [ADJ] A sickly person or animal is weak, unhealthy, and often ill. 多病的；体弱的
   - 例句：He was a sickly child who spent much of his time indoors. 他是个多病的孩子，大部分时间都在室内度过。
2. [ADJ] Sickly means weak, pale, or unpleasant. 病态的；令人不快的
   - 例句：The room was lit by a sickly yellow light. 房间里透着一股病态的黄光。

**原文补充**（100 Words）

**音标**：*(sĭk′lē) adjective*

**定义**：
1. Tending to become sick; frail:
   例：One puppy was sickly and kept apart from the others.
2. Resembling or seeming like a symptom of sickness:
   例：The sky turned a sickly green before the tornado struck.

**文学例句**：
In general, I avoided looking at myself much in the mirror, but when I did get a glimpse during those days, I could see my eyes had deep black circles around them. The rest of my face had a sickly color with my nose and the whites of my eyes looking a bloodshot red.
— Aaron Hawkins, *The Year Money Grew on Trees*

## 注意力锚
sickly 与 sick 的区分：sick = "生病的"（当前状态），sickly = "多病的/病态的"（长期特征），-ly 后缀将临时状态转化为持久特质，这一构词模式值得注意

## 类型
形容词 / 描述性 / 中性-贬义

## 正文
sickly 描述长期多病或呈现病态特征的状态，既可指人（a sickly child 经常生病的孩子），也可指外观（a sickly complexion 病态的面色），甚至可指令人不快的气味或光线（a sickly yellow light）。与 sick 的关键区分：sick 指当前生病的状态，sickly 指长期多病的特质或病态的外观。语域中性偏贬义，CEFR 约在 C1 区间。典型搭配：sickly child, sickly smile, sickly sweet。

## 强对话链接
**[[sickly]]** → frail, ailing, weakly, invalid
- frail → weak and delicate
- ailing → suffering from illness
- weakly → in a weak manner
- invalid → chronically ill/disabled
- [[sick]] → sickly 的词根，sick 指当前生病状态，sickly 指长期多病特质
- [[frail]] → sickly 的近义，frail 更侧重"虚弱脆弱"，sickly 更侧重"多病病态"
