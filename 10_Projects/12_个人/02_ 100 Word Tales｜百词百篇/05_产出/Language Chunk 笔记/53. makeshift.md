## 提纯
makeshift 指因缺乏更好选择而临时凑合使用的，核心语义是"权宜的、临时代用的"。

## 词典查证
**音标**（Cambridge Dictionary）
英 /ˈmeɪkʃɪft/　美 /ˈmeɪkʃɪft/

**核心定义**（Collins Dictionary）
1. [something made or used for a short time because nothing better is available] （临时代用的，权宜的）
   例：They built a makeshift shelter from branches and leaves.

**原文补充**（100 Words）

**音标**：*(māk′shĭft′)*

**定义**：
Serving as a temporary substitute for something else  
例：*We used the lid of the trash can as a makeshift sled.*

**文学例句**：
Each morning when they came out of their tent, they could see that other families had come to join the makeshift camp. There were no more proper tents, so the new arrivals had to make do with plastic sheets hung over chestnut tree branches and propped up with sticks.  
— Katherine Paterson, *The Day of the Pelican*

## 注意力锚
makeshift 暗含"不够理想但暂时够用"的评价色彩，与 temporary 的时间性不同，强调的是替代性和凑合性。

## 类型
定义/语域

## 正文
makeshift 是形容词，指因条件所限而临时凑合的、权宜的，也可作名词指临时替代物。与 temporary 侧重时间短暂不同，makeshift 侧重质量或适用性的妥协——不是最好的选择，但在当下可用。语域偏日常。
