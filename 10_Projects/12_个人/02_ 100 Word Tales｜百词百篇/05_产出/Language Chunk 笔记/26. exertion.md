## 提纯
exertion — 体力的消耗或努力的付出，强调用力过程本身

## 词典查证
**音标**（Cambridge Dictionary）
英 /ɪɡˈzɜːʃn/　美 /ɪɡˈzɜːrʃn/

**核心定义**（Collins Dictionary）
1. n. [physical or mental effort] （体力或脑力的消耗、努力）
   例：He was breathing hard from the exertion of climbing.
2. n. [the act of using power or influence] （行使权力或影响力）
   例：The exertion of authority was necessary.

**原文补充**（100 Words）

**音标**：(ĭg zûr′shən)

**定义**：
1. The act of working hard at something
   例：The wheelbarrow allowed us to move the rocks with very little exertion.

**文学例句**：
Gregor began to pant, but the roaches didn't show any visible signs of exertion. He had no idea how far they were going. Their destination could be a hundred miles away. Who knew how far these things could run?
— Suzanne Collins, *Gregor the Overlander*

## 注意力锚
exertion 与 effort 的区别在于它更强调"物理性的用力"而非抽象的努力，且常带有"费力"的语感

## 类型
定义/语域

## 正文
exertion 指体力或脑力的消耗，尤指需要较大力气的活动。常见搭配：physical/mental exertion, require great exertion。与 effort 相比，exertion 更聚焦于"用力"这一动作过程，而非"尝试"的意图。语域偏正式。
