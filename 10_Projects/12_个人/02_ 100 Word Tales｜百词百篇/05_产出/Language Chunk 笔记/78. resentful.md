## 提纯
resentful — 怨恨的，愤懑的，因感到被不公对待而产生的持续负面情绪

## 词典查证
**音标**（Cambridge Dictionary）
英 /rɪˈzentf(ə)l/　美 /rɪˈzentf(ə)l/

**核心定义**（Collins Dictionary）
1. [feeling angry because you have been treated unfairly] （怨恨的，愤懑的）
   例：He grew resentful of his colleague's promotion.

**原文补充**（100 Words）

**音标**：*(rĭ zĕnt′fŭl) adjective*

**定义**：
1. Angry or bitter about something someone has done to you:  
   *Daniel was resentful about not being chosen to sing in the musical.*

**文学例句**：
> Her voice had the same sugary sweet tone she had started with, but the words were bitter and resentful. All of a sudden it was like I was trying to steal something from her—something I wasn't good enough to have.  
> — Aaron Hawkins, *The Year Money Grew on Trees*

## 注意力锚
resentful 源自 resent（怨恨），-ful 后缀标记"充满…的"，与 bitter、indignant 构成"愤怒"语义场的精细区分——resentful 强调"因不公而生的持续怨恨"

## 类型
形容词 / 情感描述 / 中性-正式

## 正文
resentful 描述因感到被不公正对待而产生的怨恨和愤懑情绪，这种情绪通常是持续的、内化的，而非爆发式的。与 angry 相比，resentful 更强调"怀恨在心"的持久性；与 bitter 相比，resentful 更侧重"对特定不公的怨恨"，bitter 则更侧重"长期积累的苦涩"。语域中性偏正式，CEFR 约在 B2-C1 区间。典型搭配：feel resentful, resentful of sb/sth, resentful attitude。

## 强对话链接
**[[resentful]]** → bitter, indignant, aggrieved, embittered
- bitter → more general, long-term resentment
- indignant → righteous anger at injustice
- aggrieved → feeling wronged, legal/formal
- embittered → made bitter by experience
- [[bitter]] → resentful 的近义，bitter 更侧重长期积累的苦涩感，resentful 更侧重对不公的怨恨
- [[indignant]] → resentful 的近义，indignant 更侧重"义愤"，resentful 更侧重"个人怨恨"
