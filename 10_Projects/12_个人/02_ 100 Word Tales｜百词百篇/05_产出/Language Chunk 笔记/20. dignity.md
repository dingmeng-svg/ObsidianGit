## 词典查证
**音标**（Cambridge Dictionary）
英 /ˈdɪɡ.nə.ti/ 美 /ˈdɪɡ.nə.t̬i/

**核心定义**（Collins Dictionary）
1. 尊严：The quality of being worthy of respect
2. 庄重：A calm and serious manner

**例句**（Collins）
1. He maintained his dignity throughout the trial.
2. She accepted the award with dignity.

**原文补充**（100 Words）

**音标**：(dĭg′nĭ tē)

**定义**：
1. The fact of being or appearing worthy of respect or honor
   例：Your brother may have failed the test, but at least he kept his dignity by not cheating.

**文学例句**：
"What are you doing trespassing in my cabin?" I asked the question with as much dignity as I could muster while spitting out leaves, brushing off my clothes, and getting to my feet.
— Katherine Paterson, *Preacher's Boy*
