## 词典查证
**音标**（Cambridge Dictionary）
英 /kəmˈpæʃ.ən/ 美 /kəmˈpæʃ.ən/

**核心定义**（Collins Dictionary）
1. 同情：A feeling of sadness for someone else's suffering
2. 怜悯：The desire to help someone in trouble

**例句**（Collins）
1. She showed great compassion for the victims.
2. He felt compassion for the homeless.

**原文补充**（100 Words）

**音标**：(kəm păsh′ən)

**定义**：
1. A feeling of sharing someone else's suffering, together with a desire to help
   例：Compassion led us to volunteer at the food pantry.

**文学例句**：
Jane Sharp became a midwife because she had given birth to six children (although none of them lived), went Sundays to Mass, and had strong hands and clean fingernails. She did her job with energy and some skill, but without care, compassion, or joy.
— Karen Cushman, *The Midwife's Apprentice*
