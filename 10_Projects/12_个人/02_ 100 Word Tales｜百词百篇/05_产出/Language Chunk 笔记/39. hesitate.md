## 提纯
hesitate — 犹豫、迟疑，因不确定而不愿行动或说话

## 词典查证
**音标**（Cambridge Dictionary）
- 英式：/ˈhez.ɪ.teɪt/
- 美式：/ˈhez.ə.teɪt/

**核心定义**（Collins Dictionary）
1. **[verb] 犹豫、迟疑**： to pause before saying or doing something, because you are unsure or nervous  
   例：Don't hesitate to call me if you need help.（如果你需要帮助，不要犹豫，给我打电话。）
   
2. **[verb] 不情愿**： to be reluctant to do something  
   例：She hesitated over the decision for days.（她在决定上犹豫了好几天。）
   
3. **[verb] 停顿**： to stop temporarily; pause  
   例：He hesitated at the door before entering.（他在门口停顿了一下才进去。）

**原文补充**（100 Words）

**音标**：(hĕz′ĭ tāt′)

**定义**：
1. To be slow to act, speak, or decide because of feeling unsure; pause
   例：I hesitated before diving off the high board. Please don't hesitate to ask a question if there's something you don't understand.

**文学例句**：
She thanked the small gentleman for his trouble. He bowed, hesitated as if he meant to say something further, then seemed to think better of it, and drove away.
— Edward Eager, *Half Magic*

## 注意力锚
hesitate 的增量在于它不仅描述"停顿"的动作，更暗示内心的不确定或矛盾——这是外在行为与内在心理的连接点

## 类型
定义/语域

## 正文
hesitate 表示因不确定、担心或矛盾而暂时停止行动或说话。常见搭配：hesitate to do sth, don't hesitate to, hesitate over/about。语域中性。
