## 提纯
persuade 指说服、劝服，核心语义是"通过说理使某人同意或做某事"。

## 词典查证
**音标**（Cambridge Dictionary）
- UK: /pəˈsweɪd/
- US: /pərˈsweɪd/

**核心定义**（Collins Dictionary）
1. **v. 说服，劝服** — To persuade means to convince someone to do something. 例：She persuaded her parents to let her study abroad.
2. **v. 使相信** — To persuade also means to make someone believe something. 例：He persuaded the jury of his innocence.

**原文补充**（100 Words）

**音标**：*(pər swād′) verb*

**定义**：
1. To talk someone into doing or believing something; convince:  
   *We finally persuaded them that they were wrong.*

**文学例句**：
> More even than she hated cooked things, the mermaid hated anything sweet. Once the hunter persuaded her to try some berries: she sniffed uncertainly at them, put them in her mouth, and then spat them out, exclaiming: "They're ugly, ugly! All gummy and blurry! How can you eat them?"  
> — Randall Jarrell, *The Animal Family*

## 注意力锚
persuade 与 convince 的区别：persuade 侧重"行动"（说服去做），convince 侧重"信念"（使相信）。"persuade sb to do" vs "convince sb that"。

## 类型
定义/语域

## 正文
persuade 是动词，指说服、劝服，通过说理、情感或论证使某人同意某事或采取某行动。常见搭配 "persuade sb to do sth" 和 "persuade sb of sth"。与 convince 相比更侧重促成行动而非改变信念，与 coerce 相比依赖理性而非强制。语域通用。
