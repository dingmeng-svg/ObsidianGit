## 词典查证
**音标**（Cambridge Dictionary）
英 /brɪŋk/ 美 /brɪŋk/

**核心定义**（Collins Dictionary）
1. 边缘：The edge of a cliff or other high place
2. 临界点：The point where a new situation is about to begin

**例句**（Collins）
1. He stood on the brink of the cliff.
2. The company was on the brink of bankruptcy.

**原文补充**（100 Words）

**音标**：(brĭngk)

**定义**：
1. The upper edge of a steep place
   例：From the brink of the cliff, you can look straight down.
2. The land bordering a body of water
   例：The ball stopped rolling just at the brink of the puddle.
3. The point when an event is about to occur
   例：He's on the brink of crying.

**文学例句**：
On they all went, leading their ponies, till they were brought to a good path and so at last to the very brink of the river.
— J. R. R. Tolkien, *The Hobbit*
