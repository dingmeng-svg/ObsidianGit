## 提纯
nimble 指灵活敏捷的，核心语义是"轻巧快速的身体或思维灵活性"。

## 词典查证
**音标**（Cambridge Dictionary）
- UK: /ˈnɪmbl/
- US: /ˈnɪmbl/

**核心定义**（Collins Dictionary）
1. **adj. 灵活的，敏捷的** — Someone who is nimble can move quickly and easily. 例：The pianist's nimble fingers danced across the keys.
2. **adj. 机敏的，聪慧的** — A nimble mind thinks quickly and cleverly. 例：She has a nimble mind that solves problems creatively.

**原文补充**（100 Words）

**音标**：*(nĭm′bəl) adjective*

**定义**：
1. Moving quickly, lightly, and easily:  
   *The nimble cat jumped up on the fence.*
2. Quick and clever in thinking, learning, or answering:  
   *It takes a nimble mind to do well on a quiz show.*

**文学例句**：
> Magic shut the gates, but he could sometimes get out, if he was quick. Companies of the Wood‑elves, sometimes with the king at their head, would from time to time ride out to hunt, or to other business in the woods and in the lands to the East. Then if Bilbo was very nimble, he could slip out just behind them; though it was a dangerous thing to do.  
> — J. R. R. Tolkien, *The Hobbit*

## 注意力锚
nimble 既可描述身体灵活（nimble fingers），也可描述思维敏捷（nimble mind），这种身心双重适用性是增量价值。

## 类型
定义/语域

## 正文
nimble 是形容词，指灵活的、敏捷的，可描述身体的轻巧快速（手指灵活、脚步敏捷），也可指思维的机敏聪慧。与 agile 相比更强调轻巧感，与 quick 相比更强调优雅的灵活性。语域通用/文学。
