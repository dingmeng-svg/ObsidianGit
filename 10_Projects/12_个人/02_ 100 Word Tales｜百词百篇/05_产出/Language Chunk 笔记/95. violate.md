## 提纯
violate — 违反，侵犯，违背法律、规则、协议或权利

## 词典查证
**音标**（Cambridge Dictionary）
- 英式：/ˈvaɪ.ə.leɪt/
- 美式：/ˈvaɪ.ə.leɪt/

**核心定义**（Collins Dictionary）
1. [T] If you violate a law, rule, or agreement, you break it. 违反；违背
   - 例句：The company violated environmental regulations. 这家公司违反了环保法规。
2. [T] To violate someone's rights means to interfere with them. 侵犯（权利）
   - 例句：The police were accused of violating citizens' rights. 警方被指控侵犯公民权利。

**原文补充**（100 Words）

**音标**：*(vī′ə lāt′) verb*

**定义**：
To fail to obey a law or rule:
例：The driver was fined for violating the speed limit.

**文学例句**：
Everyone in the school knew Linda Gold. If there was ever a list with kids who had done something or won something, you could always find Linda's name near the top of it. She was blond with gray‑green eyes and a sprinkling of freckles around her nose. She also had a running battle with Miss Green, who accused her of violating the school rules by using lipstick.
— Walter Dean Myers, *Darnell Rock Reporting*

## 注意力锚
violate 与 break、breach 构成"违反"语义场，但 violate 更正式更严重，常用于法律、人权、神圣事物等语境，带有"侵犯/亵渎"的强烈语义色彩

## 类型
动词 / 法律-正式 / 贬义

## 正文
violate 指违反或侵犯法律、规则、协议、权利等，带有严重性和正式性。常见搭配：violate the law（违法）、violate human rights（侵犯人权）、violate a treaty（违背条约）、violate one's privacy（侵犯隐私）。与 break 的区分：break 更泛化更口语，violate 更正式更严重。与 breach 的区分：breach 更侧重"违约/突破"，violate 更侧重"侵犯/违背"。语域正式偏贬义，CEFR 约在 B2-C1 区间。

## 强对话链接
**[[violate]]** → break, breach, infringe, transgress
- break → fail to observe
- breach → break an agreement/law
- infringe → encroach on rights
- transgress → go beyond limits
- [[break]] → violate 的近义，break 更泛化口语，violate 更正式严重
- [[breach]] → violate 的近义，breach 更侧重违约突破，violate 更侧重侵犯违背
