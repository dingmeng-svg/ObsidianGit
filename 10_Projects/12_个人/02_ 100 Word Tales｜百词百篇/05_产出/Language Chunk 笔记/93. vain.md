## 提纯

## 词典查证
**音标**（Cambridge Dictionary）
- 英式：/veɪn/
- 美式：/veɪn/

**核心定义**（Collins Dictionary）
1. [ADJ] A vain attempt or action is one that does not achieve what you want. 徒劳的；无结果的
   - 例句：All their efforts to find him were in vain. 他们寻找他的所有努力都白费了。
2. [ADJ] Someone who is vain is too proud of their appearance or abilities. 自负的；爱慕虚荣的
   - 例句：She was too vain to admit she was wrong. 她太自负了，不肯承认自己错了。

**原文补充**（100 Words）

**音标**：*(vān) adjective*

**定义**：
1. Too proud of your appearance or accomplishments; conceited:
   例：He was a vain person who always talked about himself.
2. Unsuccessful; futile:
   例：Firefighters made a vain attempt to save the burning building.

**文学例句**：
I don't have anything to be vain about. I have no talent to show. Even if I did, I have no desire to throw myself before people for their applause.
— Rita Williams-Garcia, *One Crazy Summer*

## 注意力锚
vain 的两个义项语义完全不同：①徒劳的/无结果的（in vain）②自负的/爱慕虚荣的（vain person），需通过上下文判断，短语 in vain 是高频固定搭配

## 类型
形容词 / 多义 / 中性-贬义

## 正文
vain 有两个主要义项：①徒劳的、无结果的，常用于短语 in vain（徒劳无功）；②自负的、爱慕虚荣的，形容过分关注外貌或成就的人。两个义项语义完全不同，需通过语境判断。常见搭配：in vain（徒劳）、a vain attempt（徒劳的尝试）、a vain person（自负的人）、vain about one's appearance（对容貌虚荣）。义项①语域中性，义项②语域贬义，CEFR 约在 B2 区间。

## 强对话链接
**[[vain]]** → futile (义项①), conceited (义项②)
- futile → useless, pointless
- fruitless → without result
- conceited → having high opinion of oneself
- narcissistic → excessively self-loving
- [[futile]] → vain 义项①的近义，futile 更侧重"无望成功"，vain 更侧重"无结果"
- [[conceited]] → vain 义项②的近义，conceited 更侧重"自大"，vain 更侧重"虚荣/爱炫耀"
