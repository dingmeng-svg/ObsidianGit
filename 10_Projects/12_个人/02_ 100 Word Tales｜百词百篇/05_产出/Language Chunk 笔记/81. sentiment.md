## 提纯
sentiment — 情感，观点，情绪性意见，可指个人感受也可指群体情绪

## 词典查证
**音标**（Cambridge Dictionary）
- 英式：/ˈsen.tɪ.mənt/
- 美式：/ˈsen.tə.mənt/

**核心定义**（Collins Dictionary）
1. [C/U] A sentiment is an attitude, thought, or judgment prompted by feeling. 基于情感的态度或观点
   - 例句：Public sentiment is shifting in favor of the reform. 公众舆论正在转向支持改革。
2. [U] Sentiment is tender or romantic feeling. 柔情；感伤情绪
   - 例句：There was a sentiment of nostalgia in her voice. 她的声音中带着一丝怀旧的感伤。

**原文补充**（100 Words）

**音标**：*(sĕn′tə mənt) noun*

**定义**：
1. A tender, romantic, or passionate feeling about something:
   例：The march music stirred up their patriotic sentiment.
2. A general opinion or view:
   例：The sentiment of the community is that we need more public parks.

**文学例句**：
Mary had never possessed an animal pet of her own and had always thought she should like one. So she began to feel a slight interest in Dickon, and as she had never before been interested in any one but herself, it was the dawning of a healthy sentiment.
— Frances Hodgson Burnett, *The Secret Garden*

## 注意力锚
sentiment 与 feeling、emotion 构成"情感"语义场的三级区分：feeling 最泛化，emotion 更强烈，sentiment 则带有"观点/态度"的理性成分，常用于"公众情绪"语境

## 类型
名词 / 情感-观点 / 正式

## 正文
sentiment 有两个主要义项：①基于情感的看法或态度（patriotic sentiment, anti-war sentiment）；②柔情或感伤情绪（a sentiment of nostalgia）。与 feeling 相比，sentiment 更正式且常隐含"带有价值判断的观点"；与 emotion 相比，sentiment 更侧重"社会化、文化化的情感表达"。常见搭配：public sentiment, popular sentiment, sentiment against/in favor of。语域正式，CEFR 约在 B2-C1 区间。

## 强对话链接
**[[sentiment]]** → emotion, feeling, opinion, attitude
- emotion → stronger, more instinctive
- feeling → more general, everyday use
- opinion → more rational, less emotional
- attitude → settled way of thinking
- [[emotion]] → sentiment 的近义，emotion 更侧重本能的强烈情感，sentiment 更侧重带有观点的情感
- [[feeling]] → sentiment 的近义，feeling 更泛化更日常，sentiment 更正式更带观点性
