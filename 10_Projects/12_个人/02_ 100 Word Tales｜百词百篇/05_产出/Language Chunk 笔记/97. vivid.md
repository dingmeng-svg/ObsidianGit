## 提纯

## 词典查证
**音标**（Cambridge Dictionary）
- 英式：/ˈvɪv.ɪd/
- 美式：/ˈvɪv.ɪd/

**核心定义**（Collins Dictionary）
1. [ADJ] Vivid memories, dreams, or descriptions are very clear and detailed. 生动的；清晰的
   - 例句：She has vivid memories of her childhood in Paris. 她对在巴黎度过的童年有着清晰的记忆。
2. [ADJ] Vivid colors are bright and strong. 鲜艳的
   - 例句：The artist used vivid colors to capture the sunset. 艺术家用鲜艳的色彩描绘了日落。

**原文补充**（100 Words）

**音标**：*(vĭv′ĭd) adjective*

**定义**：
1. Bright and strong; brilliant:
   例：Isaiah's new coat is a vivid shade of blue.
2. Active; lively:
   例：You have a vivid imagination.
3. Sharp and clear:
   例：We still have vivid memories of our trip to Washington, DC.

**文学例句**：
He looked back. For a minute he thought he heard a faint muffled bark, and it seemed as though the iron tail had started to wag. Mark guessed he must have a pretty vivid imagination, all right, the way Miss Amrhein, his last year's teacher, had always said.
— Edward Eager, *Half Magic*

## 注意力锚
vivid 具有双重语义：①视觉上的"鲜艳/明亮" ②记忆/描述上的"生动/清晰"，两义共享"鲜明突出"的核心语义，这一多义性在文学描写中极为重要

## 类型
形容词 / 描述性 / 中性-褒义

## 正文
vivid 有两个主要义项：①视觉上鲜艳明亮的（vivid colors, a vivid sunset）；②记忆或描述上生动清晰的（a vivid memory, a vivid description）。两个义项共享"鲜明突出"的核心语义。与 bright 的区分：bright 更侧重"明亮"，vivid 更侧重"鲜艳/生动"。与 lively 的区分：lively 更侧重"活泼/有活力"，vivid 更侧重"鲜明/栩栩如生"。语域中性偏褒义，CEFR 约在 B2 区间。典型搭配：vivid memory, vivid description, vivid imagination, vivid colors。

## 强对话链接
**[[vivid]]** → bright, lively, graphic, colorful
- bright → giving out light, vivid
- lively → full of life, energetic
- graphic → visually clear, explicit
- colorful → rich in variety/interest
- [[bright]] → vivid 的近义，bright 更侧重明亮，vivid 更侧重鲜艳生动
- [[lively]] → vivid 的近义，lively 更侧重活泼有活力，vivid 更侧重鲜明栩栩如生
