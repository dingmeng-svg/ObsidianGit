## 提纯
pact 指正式的协议、契约，核心语义是"各方之间的正式约定或同盟"。

## 词典查证
**音标**（Cambridge Dictionary）
- UK: /pækt/
- US: /pækt/

**核心定义**（Collins Dictionary）
1. **n. 协议，契约** — A pact is a formal agreement between people or groups. 例：The two nations signed a non-aggression pact.
2. **n. 同盟，联盟** — A pact can also be an alliance or coalition. 例：They made a pact to support each other during difficult times.

**原文补充**（100 Words）

**音标**：*(păkt) noun*

**定义**：
1. A formal agreement, especially between nations; a treaty:  
   *The leaders signed a pact to ban testing of nuclear weapons.*

**文学例句**：
> Outside in the corridor, we made a pact that we wouldn't tell any of the other children about what we had seen. Mr. Snoddy had always been kind to us, and we wanted to repay him by keeping his deep dark secret to ourselves.  
> — Roald Dahl, *Danny the Champion of the World*

## 注意力锚
pact 与 treaty 的区别：pact 更通用，可指国家间或个人间的约定；treaty 专指国家间的正式条约。pact 常出现在政治语境（non-aggression pact）和私人语境（suicide pact）中。

## 类型
定义/语域

## 正文
pact 是名词，指正式的协议、契约或同盟，强调各方之间的约定和承诺。可用于国际关系（pact between nations）、商业合作或个人间的约定。与 agreement 相比更正式，与 treaty 相比适用范围更广。语域偏正式/政治。
