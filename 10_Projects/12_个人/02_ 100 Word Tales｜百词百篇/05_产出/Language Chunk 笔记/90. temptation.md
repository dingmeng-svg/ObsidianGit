## 提纯
temptation — 诱惑，引诱，指令人想做的事（尤指不该做的）

## 词典查证
**音标**（Cambridge Dictionary）
- 英式：/tempˈteɪ.ʃən/
- 美式：/tempˈteɪ.ʃən/

**核心定义**（Collins Dictionary）
1. [C/U] A temptation is a desire to do something, especially something wrong or unwise. 诱惑；引诱
   - 例句：He gave in to temptation and ate the chocolate cake. 他屈服于诱惑，吃了巧克力蛋糕。
2. [ ] Temptation is the act of tempting someone or the state of being tempted. 诱惑的行为或状态
   - 例句：She resisted temptation and stayed faithful to her diet. 她抵制住诱惑，坚持节食。

**原文补充**（100 Words）

**音标**：*(tĕmp tā′shən) noun*

**定义**：
1. The act of tempting or the state of being tempted:
   例：We gave in to temptation and ate all the cookies.
2. Something that tempts:
   例：A cool swim on a hot day was too great a temptation to resist.

**文学例句**：
"Kenny, we've done all we can and it seems the temptations are just too much for By here in Flint. So hopefully, the slower pace in Alabama will help him by removing some of those temptations."
— Christopher Paul Curtis, *The Watsons Go to Birmingham—1963*

## 注意力锚
temptation 与 temptation to do sth 的搭配结构是高频模式，且该词隐含"道德冲突"——想做的事 vs 不该做的事，这一"道德张力"是其核心语义特征

## 类型
名词 / 情感-道德 / 中性

## 正文
temptation 指强烈的想要做某事的冲动，尤指不该做或有害的事。隐含道德或理性的冲突：想要做 vs 不该做。常见搭配：resist temptation（抵制诱惑）、give in to temptation（屈服于诱惑）、the temptation to do sth。与 urge 的区分：urge 更中性，temptation 更侧重"不该做但想做的冲动"。与 attraction 的区分：attraction 更侧重"吸引力"，temptation 更侧重"引诱/试探"。语域中性，CEFR 约在 B2 区间。

## 强对话链接
**[[temptation]]** → urge, impulse, enticement, allurement
- urge → strong desire
- impulse → sudden urge
- enticement → something that attracts
- allurement → power of attraction
- [[urge]] → temptation 的近义，urge 更中性，temptation 更侧重道德冲突
- [[attraction]] → temptation 的近义，attraction 更侧重吸引力，temptation 更侧重引诱
