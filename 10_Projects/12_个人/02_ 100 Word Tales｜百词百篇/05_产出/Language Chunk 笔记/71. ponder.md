## 提纯
ponder 指深思、沉思，核心语义是"认真而持续地思考某事"。

## 词典查证
**音标**（Cambridge Dictionary）
- UK: /ˈpɒndə/
- US: /ˈpɑndər/

**核心定义**（Collins Dictionary）
1. **v. 深思，沉思** — To ponder means to think about something carefully. 例：He sat by the window, pondering the meaning of life.
2. **v. 考虑，琢磨** — Ponder implies spending time considering something seriously. 例：She pondered over the difficult decision for days.

**原文补充**（100 Words）

**音标**：*(pŏn′dər) verb*

**定义**：
1. To think about something carefully; consider:  
   *Alyssa pondered the meaning of her dream. Noah pondered over the decision.*

**文学例句**：
> Mary looked at the fire and pondered a little. She must be careful if she meant to keep her secret kingdom. She wasn't doing any harm, but if Mr. Craven found out about the open door he would be fearfully angry and get a new key and lock it up forevermore. She really could not bear that.  
> — Frances Hodgson Burnett, *The Secret Garden*

## 注意力锚
ponder 比 think 更正式、更深入，暗示一种缓慢、认真的沉思过程，常搭配 "ponder on/over" 或直接跟宾语。

## 类型
定义/语域

## 正文
ponder 是动词，指深思、沉思、认真考虑，强调对某事进行深入、持续的思考。常见搭配 "ponder a question"、"ponder over sth"。与 think 相比更正式、更深入，与 consider 相比更强调沉思的过程而非权衡，与 muse 相比更严肃。语域偏正式/文学。
