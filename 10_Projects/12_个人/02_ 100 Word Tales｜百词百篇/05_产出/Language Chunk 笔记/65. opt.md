## 提纯
opt 指选择、决定，核心语义是"在选项中做出抉择"。

## 词典查证
**音标**（Cambridge Dictionary）
- UK: /ɒpt/
- US: /ɑpt/

**核心定义**（Collins Dictionary）
1. **v. 选择，决定** — To opt means to choose one thing instead of another. 例：She opted for the earlier flight to have more time at her destination.
2. **常见搭配** — opt for sth（选择某物），opt to do sth（决定做某事），opt out（选择退出）. 例：Many employees opted out of the pension plan.

**原文补充**（100 Words）

**音标**：*(ŏpt) verb*

**定义**：
1. To make a choice or decision about something:  
   *We opted to walk instead of driving.*

**文学例句**：
> Mom immediately ordered a bratwurst with sauerkraut and peppers, even though we had just eaten lunch at home. My father opted for the homemade vanilla ice cream smothered with fresh strawberries. And Emily and I found ourselves in front of the cotton candy booth.  
> — Lisa Yee, *Millicent Min, Girl Genius*

## 注意力锚
opt 常搭配 "opt for" 或 "opt to do"，比 choose 更正式，暗示在多个选项中经过考虑后做出选择，而非随意挑选。

## 类型
定义/语域

## 正文
opt 是动词，指选择、决定，常用于 "opt for sth"（选择某物）或 "opt to do sth"（决定做某事）。与 choose 相比更正式，暗示经过权衡后的抉择；与 decide 相比更强调在选项中选取。反义搭配 "opt out" 指选择退出。语域偏正式。
