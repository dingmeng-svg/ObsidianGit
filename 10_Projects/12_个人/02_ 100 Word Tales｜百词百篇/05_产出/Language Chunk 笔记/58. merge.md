## 提纯
merge 指合为一体、融合，核心语义是"多个实体融合为单一整体，个体边界消失"。

## 词典查证
**音标**（Cambridge Dictionary）
- UK: /mɜːdʒ/
- US: /mɜrdʒ/

**核心定义**（Collins Dictionary）
1. **v. 合并，融合** — To merge is to combine or join things together into a single whole. 例：The two companies merged to form a global conglomerate.
2. **v. 汇入，并入** — In traffic, to merge means to join a lane smoothly. 例：The traffic merged into a single lane ahead.

**原文补充**（100 Words）

**音标**：*(mûrj)*

**定义**：
1. To bring two or more things together in order to form a single unit; unite  
   例：*The owners decided to merge the two companies.*
2. To come together  
   例：*The rivers run parallel before they merge.*

**文学例句**：
The cleared land gave again to forest and his voice echoed through the woodland, merging with the singing of birds, the running of a brook, the clopping of a horse's hooves.  
— Elizabeth Yates, *Amos Fortune, Free Man*

## 注意力锚
merge 与 blend 的区别：merge 强调边界消失、完全融合，blend 侧重和谐混合但可能保留各自特征。商业语境中 "merge" 特指企业合并。

## 类型
定义/语域

## 正文
merge 是动词，指两个或多个事物融合为一个整体，原有个体的独立边界消失。常见于商业（merge two companies）、交通（merge lanes）、抽象概念（merge ideas）等语境。与 combine 相比更强调完全融合而非简单组合。语域通用/商业。
