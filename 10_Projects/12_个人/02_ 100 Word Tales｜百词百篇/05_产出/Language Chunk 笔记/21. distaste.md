## 提纯
distaste — 对某事物的强烈厌恶或不悦感，带有轻微的排斥情绪

## 词典查证
**音标**（Cambridge Dictionary）
英 /dɪˈsteɪst/　美 /dɪˈsteɪst/

**核心定义**（Collins Dictionary）
1. [a feeling of not liking something or someone] （对某事物或某人的不喜欢、厌恶）
   例：She looked at the mess with distaste.

**原文补充**（100 Words）

**音标**：(dĭs tāst′)

**定义**：
1. A feeling of not liking something
   例：I looked with distaste at the plate of undercooked eggs.

**文学例句**：
When he returned to the classroom, he saw a large box with several smaller ones in front of the room. Mrs. Hamlin was crouched over the large one. With an expression of distaste on her face, she examined its contents.
— Candy Dawson Boyd, *Chevrolet Saturdays*

## 注意力锚
与 dislike 相比，distaste 更强调一种本能的、近乎生理的排斥反应，常与品味、道德判断相关

## 类型
定义/语域

## 正文
distaste 表示对某事物的不喜欢或厌恶，但比 disgust 程度轻，比 dislike 更正式。它常用于描述对某种行为、风格或观念的排斥，带有一种"不合口味"的意味。语域偏正式，常见于书面语和文学表达中。
