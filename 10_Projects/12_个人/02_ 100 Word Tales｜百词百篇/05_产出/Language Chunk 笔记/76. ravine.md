## 词典查证
**音标**（Cambridge Dictionary）
英 /rəˈviːn/　美 /rəˈviːn/

**核心定义**（Collins Dictionary）
1. [a very deep narrow valley with steep sides] （深而窄的峡谷，沟壑）
   例：The hikers descended into the dark ravine.

**原文补充**（100 Words）

**音标**：*(rə vēn′) noun*

**定义**：
1. A small valley with steep sides made by running water:  
   *The hikers descended into the ravine, where it was cool and dark.*

**文学例句**：
> This place lay on a headland a half league to the west of Coral Cove. There was a large rock on that headland and two stunted trees. Behind the rock was a clear place about ten steps across, which was sheltered from the wind, from which I could see the harbor and the ocean. A spring of water flowed from a ravine nearby.  
> — Scott O'Dell, *Island of the Blue Dolphins*

## 注意力锚
ravine 与 gorge、canyon 构成一组地貌近义词，但 ravine 强调"窄而深"且常与流水侵蚀关联，这种精确区分对地理/文学描写至关重要

## 类型
名词 / 地理术语 / 正式

## 正文
ravine 指一种深而狭窄的沟壑或峡谷，通常由河流长期侵蚀形成，两侧坡度陡峭。与 gorge 相比，ravine 通常规模更小；与 canyon 相比，ravine 更强调狭窄而非壮观。常见于自然描写和冒险叙事中，语域偏正式，CEFR 约在 C1-C2 区间。典型搭配：a deep ravine, a steep ravine, the ravine below。
