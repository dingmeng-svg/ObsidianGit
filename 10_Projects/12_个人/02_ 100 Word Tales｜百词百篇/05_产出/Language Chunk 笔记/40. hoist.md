## 提纯
hoist — 升起、吊起，用机械或绳索将重物向上提升

## 词典查证
**音标**（Cambridge Dictionary）
- 英式：/hɔɪst/
- 美式：/hɔɪst/

**核心定义**（Collins Dictionary）
1. **[verb] 升起、吊起**： to raise, lift, or pull something up, especially using ropes  
   例：They hoisted the flag at sunrise.（他们在日出时升起了旗帜。）
   
2. **[verb] 用机械提升**： to lift something heavy using mechanical equipment  
   例：The crane hoisted the container onto the ship.（起重机将集装箱吊到船上。）
   
3. **[noun] 提升装置**： an act of raising or lifting  
   例：The hoist broke down during the operation.（提升装置在操作过程中坏了。）

**原文补充**（100 Words）

**音标**：(hoist)

**定义**：
1. To lift or haul something up, especially by pulling from above
   例：The crane hoisted the beam into position.

**文学例句**：
The old man gathered his own bones and muscles as best he could and managed to hoist the kid and get him into the pickup. He laid him on the seat, bent his legs so he could close the door.
— Jerry Spinelli, *Maniac Magee*

## 注意力锚
hoist 的增量在于它暗示"用力或机械辅助的提升"——与简单的 lift 不同，hoist 常带有"重物+向上"的语境

## 类型
定义/语域

## 正文
hoist 表示用绳索、滑轮或机械将重物向上提升。常见搭配：hoist a flag/sail, hoist up, hoist with one's own petard。语域中性。
