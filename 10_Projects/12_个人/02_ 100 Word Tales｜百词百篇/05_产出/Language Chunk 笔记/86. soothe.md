## 提纯
soothe — 抚慰，缓解，使平静或减轻痛苦

## 词典查证
**音标**（Cambridge Dictionary）
- 英式：/suːð/
- 美式：/suːð/

**核心定义**（Collins Dictionary）
1. [T] If you soothe someone who is angry or upset, you calm them down. 抚慰；安慰
   - 例句：She soothed the crying child with a gentle lullaby. 她用温柔的摇篮曲安抚哭泣的孩子。
2. [T] If you soothe a pain, you reduce it. 缓解；减轻（痛苦）
   - 例句：The cream will help soothe the pain. 这种药膏有助于缓解疼痛。

**原文补充**（100 Words）

**音标**：*(s th) verb*

**定义**：
1. To make someone or something calm or quiet:
   例：The sound of the music soothed the restless baby.
2. To make something less painful; relieve:
   例：Warm tea will soothe your sore throat.

**文学例句**：
And off the midwife ran, up to the manor where warm fires blazed and the laboring mother was soothed with wine and syrups and kind words. Alyce turned back to the dark, cold, nearly empty cottage, took a deep breath, and went in.
— Karen Cushman, *The Midwife's Apprentice*

## 注意力锚
soothe 与 comfort、calm 构成"安抚"语义场，但 soothe 强调"缓解痛苦/焦虑的动作过程"，常用于身体疼痛、情绪激动、皮肤刺激等具体语境

## 类型
动词 / 情感-医疗 / 中性

## 正文
soothe 指通过安抚或治疗来减轻痛苦、焦虑或不适。既可指情绪安抚（soothe a crying baby, soothe sb's fears），也可指身体疼痛缓解（soothe a sore throat, soothing cream）。与 comfort 的区分：comfort 更侧重"给予安慰和支持"，soothe 更侧重"缓解痛苦或紧张"。与 calm 的区分：calm 更侧重"使平静"，soothe 更侧重"缓解不适"。语域中性，CEFR 约在 B2 区间。典型搭配：soothe sb's nerves, soothing voice, soothe the pain。

## 强对话链接
**[[soothe]]** → comfort, calm, placate, pacify
- comfort → give emotional support
- calm → make peaceful, quiet
- placate → make less angry
- pacify → bring peace to, quell
