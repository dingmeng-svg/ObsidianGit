## 提纯
nape 指颈背、后颈，核心语义是"头部后方的颈部区域"。

## 词典查证
**音标**（Cambridge Dictionary）
- UK: /neɪp/
- US: /neɪp/

**核心定义**（Collins Dictionary）
1. **n. 颈背，后颈** — The nape is the back of the neck. 例：She felt a cold breeze on the nape of her neck.
2. **常见搭配** — "the nape of the neck" is the standard phrase. 例：He kissed the nape of her neck gently.

**原文补充**（100 Words）

**音标**：*(nāp) noun*

**定义**：
1. The back of the neck:  
   *Mother cats carry their kittens by the nape of the neck.*

**文学例句**：
> I watch the reverend from behind. His thick shoulders force the seams of his black suit. As soon as the reverend turns to Luke 2:1–20, his whole stance changes. All of him goes tense. His hand clamps the back of his neck. Here we are in this chilly church, and the reverend wipes his nape with his hankie.  
> — Andrea Davis Pinkney, *Bird in a Box*

## 注意力锚
nape 是一个精确的身体部位词，比 "back of the neck" 更简洁。文学中常与触觉描写关联（kiss the nape, hair on the nape），带有亲密或敏感的联想。

## 正文
nape 是名词，指颈背、后颈，即头部后方与肩膀之间的颈部区域。常见搭配 "the nape of the neck"，在文学描写中常与亲密触觉、发型描述或寒冷感受关联。语域偏文学/解剖。
