## 提纯
recipient — 接收者，受领者，指接受某物（信件、奖项、捐赠等）的人或组织

## 词典查证
**音标**（Cambridge Dictionary）
英 /rɪˈsɪpiənt/　美 /rɪˈsɪpiənt/

**核心定义**（Collins Dictionary）
1. [a person who receives something] （接收者，受领者）
   例：The recipient of the letter was not at home.
2. [a person or organization that receives a prize, grant, etc.] （奖项/捐赠获得者）
   例：She was the recipient of numerous awards.

**原文补充**（100 Words）

**音标**：*(rĭ sĭp′ē ənt) noun*

**定义**：
1. Someone who receives something:  
   *She has been the recipient of many awards for her music.*

**文学例句**：
> I did not speak to him for the rest of the day, and when he left the pool to return to the condo for lunch, I did not go with him. I thought that it would do him good to know how it felt to be the recipient rather than the giver of silence.  
> — E. L. Konigsburg, *The View from Saturday*

## 注意力锚
recipient 是 giver/donor 的对偶词，掌握这对关系可同时理解"给予-接受"语义场；其 -ent 后缀标记"执行动作的人"这一构词模式

## 类型
名词 / 正式 / 行政-法律

## 正文
recipient 指接受某物的人或组织，常用于正式语境：信件收件人（recipient of the letter）、奖项获得者（recipient of the Nobel Prize）、捐赠受领者（recipient of the grant）。与 receiver 相比，recipient 更正式、更常用于书面语，且强调"被授予/被给予"的被动接受关系。语域正式，CEFR 约在 B2 区间。典型搭配：the recipient of, award recipient, grant recipient。

## 强对话链接
**[[recipient]]** → receiver, donee, beneficiary, grantee
- receiver → more general, can refer to devices
- donee → legal term for gift recipient
- beneficiary → recipient of benefits/will
- grantee → legal term for grant recipient
- [[donor]] → recipient 的反义对偶，donor 给予，recipient 接收
- [[receiver]] → recipient 的近义，但 receiver 更口语化，也可指"接收器"等物理设备
