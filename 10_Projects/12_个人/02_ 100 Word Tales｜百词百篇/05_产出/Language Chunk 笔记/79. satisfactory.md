## 提纯
satisfactory — 令人满意的，达到可接受标准的，但未必出色

## 词典查证
**音标**（Cambridge Dictionary）
英 /ˌsætɪsˈfækt(ə)ri/　美 /ˌsætɪsˈfæktɔːri/

**核心定义**（Collins Dictionary）
1. [good enough for a particular purpose] （令人满意的，达到可接受标准的）
   例：The results were satisfactory but not outstanding.
2. [acceptable] （可接受的，合格的）
   例：His performance was rated as satisfactory.

**原文补充**（100 Words）

**音标**：*(săt′ĭs făk′tə rē) adjective*

**定义**：
1. Good enough but not the best; adequate:  
   *Your work was satisfactory, but I know that you can do better.*

**文学例句**：
> The Directors thought hard, did much scratching of their heads and made many ridiculous suggestions, but were unable to think of any satisfactory ideas on such short notice.  
> — William Pene du Bois, *The Twenty‑One Balloons*

## 注意力锚
satisfactory 与 satisfying 的区分是关键：satisfactory = "达标/可接受"（偏客观评价），satisfying = "令人愉悦/满足"（偏主观感受），这一区分在学术和职场语境中极为重要

## 类型
形容词 / 评价性 / 正式

## 正文
satisfactory 指达到可接受的标准或要求，但未必令人印象深刻或愉悦。常用于正式评价语境：考试成绩（a satisfactory result）、工作表现（satisfactory performance）、产品质量（satisfactory condition）。与 satisfying 的关键区别：satisfactory 是"合格"的客观判断，satisfying 是"满足"的主观感受。语域正式，CEFR 约在 B2 区间。典型搭配：satisfactory result, satisfactory explanation, satisfactory progress。

## 强对话链接
**[[satisfactory]]** → acceptable, adequate, sufficient, passable
- acceptable → meets minimum standards
- adequate → enough for purpose
- sufficient → enough quantity/quality
- passable → barely acceptable
- [[satisfying]] → satisfactory 的近义但语义不同，satisfying 强调主观满足感，satisfactory 强调客观达标
- [[acceptable]] → satisfactory 的近义，acceptable 更宽泛，satisfactory 更侧重"满足特定标准"
