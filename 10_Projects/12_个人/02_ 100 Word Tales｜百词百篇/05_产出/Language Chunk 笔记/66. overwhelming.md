## 提纯
overwhelming 指压倒性的、势不可挡的，核心语义是"力量或程度大到难以抵抗或应对"。

## 词典查证
**音标**（Cambridge Dictionary）
- UK: /ˌəʊvəˈwelmɪŋ/
- US: /ˌoʊvərˈwelmɪŋ/

**核心定义**（Collins Dictionary）
1. **adj. 压倒性的，势不可挡的** — Something overwhelming is too great or strong to be resisted. 例：The evidence against him was overwhelming.
2. **adj. 极大的，巨大的** — Overwhelming also means very great in amount or degree. 例：She received overwhelming support from her community.

**原文补充**（100 Words）

**音标**：*(ō′vər wĕl′mĭng) adjective*

**定义**：
1. Easily defeating or overpowering an enemy or opponent:  
   *Our team's overwhelming offense brought us victory.*
2. Affecting someone very strongly; too much for someone:  
   *The humidity was overwhelming—I just had to lie down.*

**文学例句**：
> Neftalí reached out and hugged Mamadre's neck tight. He wanted to tell her how much he loved her. He wanted to tell her he was sorry for being angry with her and not talking to her. But his overwhelming emotions stood in the way of his words.  
> — Pam Muñoz Ryan, *The Dreamer*

## 注意力锚
overwhelming 可用于正面（overwhelming joy, overwhelming support）和负面（overwhelming debt, overwhelming grief）语境，这种情感极性的双向适用性是增量价值。

## 类型
定义/语域

## 正文
overwhelming 是形容词，指压倒性的、势不可挡的，强调力量、数量或程度大到难以抵抗或应对。可描述情感（overwhelming relief）、证据（overwhelming evidence）、多数（overwhelming majority）等。与 immense 侧重"大"不同，overwhelming 更强调"难以抵抗"。语域通用/正式。
