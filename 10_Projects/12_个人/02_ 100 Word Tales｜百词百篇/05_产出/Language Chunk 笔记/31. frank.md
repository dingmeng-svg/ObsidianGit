## 提纯
frank — 直率的、坦诚的，不掩饰真实想法或情感

## 词典查证
**音标**（Cambridge Dictionary）
- 英式：/fræŋk/
- 美式：/fræŋk/

**核心定义**（Collins Dictionary）
1. **[adjective] 坦率的、直言不讳的**： honest and direct in speech or writing  
   例：To be frank, I don't think this plan will work.（坦白说，我认为这个计划行不通。）
   
2. **[adjective] 真诚的、不掩饰的**： open and sincere  
   例：She gave a frank assessment of the situation.（她对形势进行了坦诚的评估。）
   
3. **[verb] 免费邮寄**：to stamp a mark on (a letter, etc.) showing that postage has been paid  
   例：The letter was franked and ready for delivery.（信件已盖邮戳，准备投递。）

**原文补充**（100 Words）

**音标**：(frăngk)

**定义**：
1. Free and open in expressing thoughts and feelings; honest
   例：Give me your frank opinion of my haircut.

**文学例句**：
Yuki liked Mr. Toda better than the seminary students because he was more open about his feelings. If he liked you, he let you know, and if he didn't, he was equally frank. Yuki liked people like that. She knew that the old man liked her too.
— Yoshiko Uchida, *Journey to Topaz*

## 注意力锚
frank 与 honest 的区别在于它侧重"表达方式上的直接"，而非"道德上的诚实"——这是语用层面的增量

## 类型
定义/语域

## 正文
frank 表示说话或表达时不隐藏、不掩饰，直接说出真实想法。常见搭配：to be frank with you, frank discussion, frank admission。既可褒义（赞扬坦诚）也可贬义（显得冒犯）。语域中性。
