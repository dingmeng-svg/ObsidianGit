## 提纯
intent — 意图、目的（名词）；专注的（形容词）

## 词典查证
**音标**（Cambridge Dictionary）
- 英式：/ɪnˈtent/
- 美式：/ɪnˈtent/

**核心定义**（Collins Dictionary）
1. **[noun, uncountable] 意图、目的**： intention; purpose  
   例：What was his intent in doing this?（他做这件事的意图是什么？）
   
2. **[adjective] 专注的、热切的**： giving careful attention to something  
   例：He was intent on finishing the work.（他专注于完成工作。）
   
3. **[phrase] to all intents and purposes**： in all important respects; practically  
   例：To all intents and purposes, the project is complete.（实际上，项目已经完成了。）

**原文补充**（100 Words）

**音标**：*(ĭn tĕnt′)*

**定义**：
**Noun:** A purpose or aim; an intention  
例：*Was it your intent to start an argument?*

**Adjective:**
1. Having the mind or thoughts set on a goal; determined  
   例：*He is intent on finishing the marathon.*
2. Showing close attention  
   例：*The judge listened with an intent expression on her face.*

**文学例句**：
I would have liked to follow him myself, but I would quickly have been missed and so had to remain in camp. How was I to find out Seth's intent and the reason he was keeping company with a wasp?  
— Mark Copeland, *The Bundle at Blackthorpe Heath*

## 注意力锚
intent 的增量在于它同时跨越名词和形容词，且"名词=意图，形容词=专注"的语义延伸值得关注

## 类型
定义/语域

## 正文
intent 作名词表示"意图、目的"（to all intents and purposes），作形容词表示"专注的、热切的"（intent on/upon）。语域正式。
