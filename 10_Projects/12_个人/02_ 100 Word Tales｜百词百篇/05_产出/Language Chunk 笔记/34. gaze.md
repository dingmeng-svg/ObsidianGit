## 提纯
gaze — 凝视，长时间地、专注地看某物

## 词典查证
**音标**（Cambridge Dictionary）
- 英式：/ɡeɪz/
- 美式：/ɡeɪz/

**核心定义**（Collins Dictionary）
1. **[verb] 凝视**： to look long and steadily at something  
   例：She gazed at the stars in wonder.（她惊奇地凝视着星星。）
   
2. **[noun] 凝视**： a steady, intent look  
   例：His gaze remained fixed on the horizon.（他的目光始终注视着地平线。）
   
3. **[noun] 注视的目光**： a way of looking at something  
   例：She met his gaze without flinching.（她毫不退缩地迎接他的目光。）

**原文补充**（100 Words）

**音标**：(gāz) verb & noun

**定义**：
1. Verb: To look at something steadily and for a long time
   例：They gazed in wonder at the high mountains.
2. Noun: A long, steady look
   例：The judge fixed her gaze on the defendant.

**文学例句**：
Mari walks up the slight incline behind the trailer to the field where the cows graze. The moon is on the wane, but it's still a pie with half its pieces left, so there is enough light to watch her by. Midfield, she stops and gazes up at the sky, slowly turning west, south, east, full circle.
— Julia Alvarez, *Return to Sender*

## 注意力锚
gaze 与 look/watch 的区别在于它强调"长时间+专注+情感投入"——不只是视觉动作，更是心理状态的外显

## 类型
定义/语域

## 正文
gaze 表示长时间、专注地凝视某物，常带有思考、欣赏、痴迷等情感色彩。常见搭配：gaze at/on/into, steady gaze, admiring gaze。与 stare 相比，gaze 更柔和、更有情感投入；与 glance 相比，gaze 更持久。语域中性偏文学。

## 强对话链接
