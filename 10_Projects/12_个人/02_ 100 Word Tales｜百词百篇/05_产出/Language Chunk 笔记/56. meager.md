## 提纯
meager 指数量少且不足的，核心语义是"令人失望的匮乏"。

## 词典查证
**音标**（Cambridge Dictionary）
- UK: /ˈmiɡə/ (also meagre)
- US: /ˈmiɡər/

**核心定义**（Collins Dictionary）
1. **adj. 贫乏的，不足的** — Something that is meager is small in amount or quality, and not enough. 例：They survived on a meager diet of rice and beans.
2. **adj. 微薄的** — A meager amount is disappointingly small. 例：He received a meager salary for his hard work.

**原文补充**（100 Words）

**音标**：*(mē′gər)*

**定义**：
Small in quantity; barely enough  
例：*Kendra complained about her meager allowance.*

**文学例句**：
The most relentless of his new fears was that they would starve. Now that they had left the cultivated fields behind them, it was almost impossible to find food. They finished the meager store of potatoes and carrots they had saved from the last agricultural area, and now they were always hungry.  
— Lois Lowry, *The Giver*

## 注意力锚
meager 带有"不够、不足"的负面评价色彩，不仅是数量少，更暗示这种少令人不满或难以维持。英式拼写为 meagre。

## 类型
定义/语域

## 正文
meager 是形容词，指数量少且不足的、贫乏的，常描述收入、食物、资源等不足以满足需要的情况。与 scarce 侧重"稀少"不同，meager 更强调"不够用"的负面评价。语域偏正式/文学。
