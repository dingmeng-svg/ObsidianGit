## 词典查证
**音标**（Cambridge Dictionary）
英 /əˈsend/ 美 /əˈsend/

**核心定义**（Collins Dictionary）
1. 上升：To go up or climb up
2. 登基：To become king or queen

**例句**（Collins）
1. He ascended the stairs slowly.
2. The prince ascended to the throne.

**原文补充**（100 Words）

**音标**：(ə sĕnd′)

**定义**：
1. To go up or move up
   例：The climbers ascended the mountain. The balloon ascended into the sky.

**文学例句**：
She led me up some wide stairs. As my eyes got used to the gloom I made out the shapes of the boarded windows, of dark doorways and broad landings. We ascended three stairways, passed three landings.
— David Almond, *Skellig*
