## 词典查证
**音标**（Cambridge Dictionary）
英 /ˈkɒn.tent/ 美 /ˈkɑːn.tent/ (名词); 英 /kənˈtent/ 美 /kənˈtent/ (形容词)

**核心定义**（Collins Dictionary）
1. 内容：The things inside something
2. 满足：Happy and satisfied

**例句**（Collins）
1. The content of the book is fascinating.
2. He seemed content with his life.

**原文补充**（100 Words）

**音标**：(kən tĕnt′)

**定义**：
1. Adjective: Happy with things the way they are; satisfied
   例：I wasn't content with the drawing I made.
2. Verb: To make someone content
   例：The young boy contented himself by playing with his mother's phone.

**文学例句**：
It was not just an island. It was the island, waiting for them. It was their island. With an island like that within sight, who could be content to live on the mainland and sleep in a bed at night?
— Arthur Ransome, *Swallows and Amazons*
