## 提纯
expanse — 广阔的区域或空间，强调延伸的广度

## 词典查证
**音标**（Cambridge Dictionary）
- 英式：/ɪkˈspæns/
- 美式：/ɪkˈspæns/

**核心定义**（Collins Dictionary）
1. **[countable] 广阔的区域**：a wide and open area of land, sea, or sky  
   例：The vast expanse of the desert stretched endlessly before us.（广阔的沙漠在我们面前无尽延伸。）
   
2. **[singular] 广阔、辽阔**：the amount of space that something covers  
   例：We looked out at the expanse of the ocean.（我们眺望着广阔的海洋。）

**原文补充**（100 Words）

**音标**：(ĭk spăns′)

**定义**：
1. A wide and open area
   例：The Sahara is a vast expanse of desert.

**文学例句**：
He had never hunted tigers in India, or climbed the peaks of the Himalayas, or dived for pearls in the South Seas. Above all, he had never seen the Poles. That was what he regretted most of all. He had never seen those great shining white expanses of ice and snow.
— Richard and Florence Atwater, *Mr. Popper's Penguins*

## 注意力锚
expanse 的增量在于它不仅是"大"，更是"向远处延伸"——带有一种视觉上的开阔感和方向性

## 类型
定义/语域

## 正文
expanse 指广阔、辽阔的区域，尤指天空、海洋、沙漠等自然景观。常见搭配：a vast/wide expanse of, the expanse of sky/ocean。与 area 相比，expanse 更强调视觉上的开阔和延伸感，带有文学色彩。语域正式偏文学。
