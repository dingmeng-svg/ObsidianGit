## 提纯
keepsake 指为纪念某人或某事而保留的小物件，核心语义是"情感承载的纪念品"。

## 词典查证
**音标**（Cambridge Dictionary）
英 /ˈkiːpseɪk/　美 /ˈkiːpseɪk/

**核心定义**（Collins Dictionary）
1. [a small item kept in memory of the person who gave it or of the event] （纪念品，留念物）
   例：She kept the locket as a keepsake of her grandmother.

**原文补充**（100 Words）

**音标**：*(kēp′sāk′)*

**定义**：
Something that is kept in memory of a person or an occasion  
例：*Jessica saved her grandmother's favorite coffee mug as a keepsake.*

**文学例句**：
Children were not allowed to bring any keepsakes with them, although some managed to smuggle a beloved photograph or locket on the journey west.  
— Andrea Warren, *Orphan Train Rider*

## 注意力锚
keepsake 强调"保留以纪念"的意图性，比 souvenir 更具私人情感色彩，不限于旅行纪念。

## 类型
定义/语域

## 正文
keepsake 是名词，指为纪念某人或某段经历而特意保存的小物品，如照片、信件、饰品等。与 souvenir 偏向旅行购买不同，keepsake 更侧重个人情感联结和记忆保存的意图，语域偏正式或感伤。
