## 提纯
massive 指体积大、数量多或影响深远的，核心语义是"超出常规的巨大规模"。

## 词典查证
**音标**（Cambridge Dictionary）
- UK: /ˈmæsɪv/
- US: /ˈmæsɪv/

**核心定义**（Collins Dictionary）
1. **adj. 巨大的，大规模的** — Something that is massive is very large in size, amount, or degree. 例：The earthquake caused massive destruction across the region.
2. **adj. 厚重的，粗大的** — Massive objects are heavy and solid. 例：A massive rock blocked the path.

**原文补充**（100 Words）

**音标**：*(măs′ĭv)*

**定义**：
Large, heavy, and solid; bulky  
例：*A massive boulder blocked the road.*

**文学例句**：
The ride was noisy, bumpy, and thrilling. When they stopped for a light before going onto the freeway, Joey was amazed that the truck filled the entire lane. At the same time, he fretted about how the massive machine would handle the speed of the freeway.  
— Candy Dawson Boyd, *Chevrolet Saturdays*

## 注意力锚
massive 在现代英语中常用于抽象语境（massive impact, massive change），从物理体积延伸到程度和影响力，这种隐喻用法是增量价值。

## 类型
定义/语域

## 正文
massive 是形容词，指极大的、大规模的、厚重的，可描述物理体积（massive rock）、数量规模（massive data）或抽象影响（massive impact）。与 huge 相比更强调厚重感和分量感，与 enormous 相比更常用于正式语境。语域通用。
