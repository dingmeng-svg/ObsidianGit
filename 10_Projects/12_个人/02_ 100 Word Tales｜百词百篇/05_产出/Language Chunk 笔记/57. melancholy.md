## 提纯
melancholy 指一种持久的、安静的悲伤，核心语义是"深沉而无明确原因的忧愁"。

## 词典查证
**音标**（Cambridge Dictionary）
- UK: /ˈmelənkəli/
- US: /ˈmelənkɑli/

**核心定义**（Collins Dictionary）
1. **n. 忧郁，悲伤** — Melancholy is a feeling of sadness and depression. 例：A sense of melancholy hung over the abandoned village.
2. **adj. 忧郁的，令人悲伤的** — Something melancholy makes you feel sad. 例：The music had a melancholy tone that moved everyone.

**原文补充**（100 Words）

**音标**：*(mĕl′ən kŏl′ē)*

**定义**：
**Adjective:** Sad; gloomy  
例：*The violinist played a melancholy song.*

**Noun:** Low spirits; sadness  
例：*The sale of my grandparents' farm filled me with melancholy.*

**文学例句**：
Now there was nothing left but to get ready to leave Arundel. They had to organize and pack and clean, all those melancholy end‑of‑vacation chores that take so much longer than they should.  
— Jeanne Birdsall, *The Penderwicks*

## 注意力锚
melancholy 与 sadness 不同——它更持久、更安静、更具美学意味，常带有一种诗意的沉思感，而非剧烈的情感痛苦。

## 类型
定义/语域

## 正文
melancholy 是形容词和名词，指一种深沉的、持久的、安静的忧愁或悲伤，往往没有明确的直接原因，带有沉思和诗意的色彩。作名词时常与 a 连用（a melancholy），作形容词可描述情绪、氛围、音乐等。语域偏文学/正式。
