## 提纯
mingle 指混合、交际，核心语义是"不同元素或人群的自由交织"。

## 词典查证
**音标**（Cambridge Dictionary）
- UK: /ˈmɪŋɡl/
- US: /ˈmɪŋɡl/

**核心定义**（Collins Dictionary）
1. **v. 混合，交织** — To mingle means to mix or combine things together. 例：The smell of coffee mingled with the scent of fresh bread.
2. **v. 交际，交往** — To mingle also means to move around and talk to people at a social event. 例：She mingled with guests at the party.

**原文补充**（100 Words）

**音标**：*(mĭng′gəl)*

**定义**：
1. To mix or become mixed; combine  
   例：*The smell of coffee mingled with the smell of fried eggs in the kitchen.*
2. To join in company with others  
   例：*We mingled with the crowd during the play's intermission.*

**文学例句**：
As he entered the water clouds crossed the sun's face and great shadows slid and mingled over the water of the pool about him. He crossed to the far bank, shuddering with cold but walking slow and erect as he should through that icy, living water.  
— Ursula K. Le Guin, *A Wizard of Earthsea*

## 注意力锚
mingle 的社交用法（mingle with guests）是增量价值——从物质混合到人际交往的隐喻延伸，暗示轻松自然的互动而非正式交流。

## 类型
定义/语域

## 正文
mingle 是动词，指不同事物混合在一起，或人在社交场合中与他人交往互动。物质义指气味、声音、液体等的交织混合；社交义指在聚会等场合与人自由交谈。与 mix 相比更强调自然、轻松的交织感。语域通用。
