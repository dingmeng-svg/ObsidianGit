## 提纯
imperceptibly — 难以察觉地、细微地，变化微小到几乎无法感知

## 词典查证
**音标**（Cambridge Dictionary）
- 英式：/ˌɪm.pəˈsep.tə.bli/
- 美式：/ˌɪm.pɚˈsep.tə.bli/

**核心定义**（Collins Dictionary）
1. **[adverb] 难以察觉地**： in a way that is so slight or gradual that it cannot be noticed  
   例：The light faded almost imperceptibly.（光线几乎难以察觉地逐渐消失。）
   
2. **[adverb] 细微地**： so slowly or slightly as to be barely noticeable  
   例：Her attitude changed imperceptibly over time.（她的态度随时间细微地变化。）
   
3. **[adverb] 不知不觉地**： in a way that cannot be perceived by the senses  
   例：The shadows lengthened imperceptibly as the day wore on.（随着白天过去，阴影不知不觉地变长了。）

**原文补充**（100 Words）

**音标**：*(ĭm′pər sĕp′tə blē)*

**定义**：
In a way that is impossible to notice  
例：*From one day to the next, the pond looked the same, but we could tell over time that it was drying up imperceptibly.*

**文学例句**：
He leaned back a little and pushed—almost imperceptibly—against the door with his shoulders: it opened sweetly, in its silent way. Not one of the men had looked up.  
— Mary Norton, *The Borrowers*

## 注意力锚
imperceptibly 的增量在于"过程性+否定性"——描述渐进式变化且强调"无法感知"，常用于文学或学术语境

## 类型
定义/语域

## 正文
imperceptibly 副词，表示以难以察觉的方式发生，常描述缓慢、细微的变化。常见搭配：change/imperceptibly, almost imperceptibly, imperceptibly slow。语域正式偏文学。
