## 提纯
stern — 严厉的，严峻的，形容表情、态度或要求严格而不妥协

## 词典查证
**音标**（Cambridge Dictionary）
- 英式：/stɜːn/
- 美式：/stɜːrn/

**核心定义**（Collins Dictionary）
1. [ADJ] Stern words or actions are very severe and show disapproval. 严厉的；严峻的
   - 例句：He gave me a stern warning. 他给了我严厉的警告。
2. [ADJ] A stern person has a serious, severe expression. 表情严肃的
   - 例句：Her father was a stern man who rarely smiled. 她父亲是个严厉的人，很少笑。

**原文补充**（100 Words）

**音标**：*(stûrn) adjective*

**定义**：
Serious or strict:
例：The lifeguard gave us a stern warning against swimming too far from the shore.

**文学例句**：
You might think, if you didn't know him well, that he was a stern and serious man. He wasn't. He was actually a wildly funny person. What made him appear so serious was the fact that he never smiled with his mouth. He did it all with his eyes. He had brilliant blue eyes and when he thought of something funny, his eyes would flash and, if you looked carefully, you could actually see a tiny little golden spark dancing in the middle of each eye. But the mouth never moved.
— Roald Dahl, *Danny the Champion of the World*

## 注意力锚
stern 与 strict、severe 构成"严厉"语义场，但 stern 更侧重"表情/态度的严肃严厉"，常用于权威人物（父母、老师、法官），带有"不苟言笑/要求严格"的语义特征

## 类型
形容词 / 描述性 / 正式-中性

## 正文
stern 描述严厉、严峻且不妥协的态度或表情，常用于权威人物：严厉的表情（stern expression）、严厉的警告（stern warning）、严厉的父母（stern parents）。与 strict 的区分：strict 更侧重"规则严格"，stern 更侧重"态度严厉"。与 severe 的区分：severe 更侧重"严酷/严厉程度高"，stern 更侧重"严肃不妥协的态度"。语域正式偏中性，CEFR 约在 B2 区间。典型搭配：stern look, stern voice, stern warning。

## 强对话链接
**[[stern]]** → strict, severe, harsh, austere
- strict → demanding obedience to rules
- severe → very strict, harsh
- harsh → cruel, unkind
- austere → severe, self-disciplined
- [[strict]] → stern 的近义，strict 更侧重规则严格，stern 更侧重态度严厉
- [[severe]] → stern 的近义，severe 更侧重严厉程度，stern 更侧重严肃态度
