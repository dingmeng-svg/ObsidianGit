## 提纯
pandemonium 指大混乱、喧嚣，核心语义是"极度无序的喧闹和混乱"。

## 词典查证
**音标**（Cambridge Dictionary）
- UK: /ˌpændəˈməʊniəm/
- US: /ˌpændəˈmoʊniəm/

**核心定义**（Collins Dictionary）
1. **n. 大混乱，喧嚣** — Pandemonium is a state of wild and noisy disorder. 例：Pandemonium broke out when the referee disallowed the goal.
2. **n. 极度混乱的局面** — It describes a situation where there is no control or order. 例：The announcement caused pandemonium in the crowded room.

**原文补充**（100 Words）

**音标**：*(păn′də mō′nē əm) noun*

**定义**：
1. A condition or scene of noisy confusion:  
   *There was pandemonium when the doors to the concert opened and the crowd rushed in.*

**文学例句**：
> Below was pandemonium. It seemed as though half of Helsinki's inhabitants had already managed to launch themselves in various crafts, and a veritable flotilla was heading toward the explosion site, led by a coast guard vessel, two powerful outboards churning at its stern, nose up for speed.  
> — Eoin Colfer, *Artemis Fowl: The Time Paradox*

## 注意力锚
pandemonium 词源来自 Milton《失乐园》中地狱之都 Pandemonium（万魔殿），从"恶魔集会之所"到"大混乱"的语义演变是增量价值。

## 正文
pandemonium 是名词，指大混乱、喧嚣、极度无序的状态，常描述人群失控、噪音震耳的场景。词源来自 Milton《失乐园》中的地狱之都名，字面义为"所有恶魔之地"。与 chaos 相比更强调噪音和喧闹，与 uproar 相比程度更强。语域偏正式/戏剧化。
