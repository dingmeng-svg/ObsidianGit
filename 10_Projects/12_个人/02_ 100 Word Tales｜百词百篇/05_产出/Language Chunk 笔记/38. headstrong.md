## 提纯
headstrong — 固执的、刚愎自用的，不听劝告

## 词典查证
**音标**（Cambridge Dictionary）
- 英式：/ˈhed.strɒŋ/
- 美式：/ˈhed.strɔːŋ/

**核心定义**（Collins Dictionary）
1. **[adjective] 固执的、刚愎自用的**： very determined to do what you want without taking advice  
   例：Her headstrong nature often led her into trouble.（她固执的性格经常让她陷入麻烦。）
   
2. **[adjective] 任性的**： not listening to other people's advice  
   例：He was a headstrong child who refused to follow rules.（他是一个固执的孩子，拒绝遵守规则。）
   
3. **[adjective] 自作主张的**： determined to have your own way  
   例：The headstrong decision cost him dearly.（这个自作主张的决定让他付出了沉重的代价。）

**原文补充**（100 Words）

**音标**：(hĕd′strông′)

**定义**：
1. Determined to have your own way; stubborn
   例：Jacob is so headstrong that it's useless to argue with him.

**文学例句**：
Lots of other mill girls were jealous of her beauty and said spiteful things to and about her, but she only tossed her head and laughed in their faces. She was as headstrong and smart as she was handsome.
— Patricia Beatty, *Turn Homeward, Hannalee*

## 注意力锚
headstrong 的增量在于它的构词透明度（head + strong）——"头脑强硬"即固执，这种形象化的构词有助于记忆

## 类型
定义/语域

## 正文
headstrong 形容人固执己见、不听劝告，坚持按自己的方式行事。常见搭配：headstrong child/personality, be headstrong about。比 stubborn 更强调"自作主张"和"冲动"。语域中性偏贬义。
