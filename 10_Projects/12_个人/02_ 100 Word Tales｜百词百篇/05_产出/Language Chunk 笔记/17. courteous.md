## 词典查证
**音标**（Cambridge Dictionary）
英 /ˈkɜː.tʃəs/ 美 /ˈkɜːr.tʃəs/

**核心定义**（Collins Dictionary）
1. 有礼貌的：Polite and respectful
2. 谦恭的：Having good manners

**例句**（Collins）
1. He was courteous to everyone he met.
2. A courteous reply was appreciated.

**原文补充**（100 Words）

**音标**：(kûr′tē əs)

**定义**：
1. Considerate toward other people; polite
   例：If you step on someone's toe, the courteous thing to do is apologize.

**文学例句**：
His face was quite calm and entirely courteous—but it was the distant, formal courtesy he always drew on like armor when he had to deal with people he disliked.
— Elizabeth Marie Pope, *The Sherwood Ring*
